## 1. What Hark is: company, founders, dates, funding, platforms, pricing

### Takeaway
Hark (Hark Labs Inc.) is an AI lab led by Brett Adcock (Figure AI founder), founded late 2025, with design led by ex-Apple designer Abidur Chowdhury. It raised a reported $700M Series A at $6B in May 2026, shipped the "Handoff" computer-use agent on 2026-08-05 and the consumer app "Hark Pro" on 2026-10-06 (web, iOS, Android). It is free with no ads. Paid tiers only raise usage limits. Dedicated hardware is planned for 2027.

## 2. Proactivity: what it does unasked, triggers, surfaces, cadence, opt-in

### Takeaway
Hark's proactivity is mainly a **pull surface, not push chat**. A "Home" screen holds **Action Buttons**: one-tap task cards Hark generates from email, conversations and connected accounts. Hark explicitly frames them as "the evolution of notifications" ("Notifications give you problems. Action Buttons give you solutions."). Multi-step tasks are **paced**, so follow-ups arrive at the moment they are needed rather than all at once. Users can also create **Scheduled Tasks** and **Specialties** (memorized workflows). Acting autonomously on a proactive suggestion requires permission. Hark has published no cadence, trigger list or notification-settings details.

### Cited Findings
- Home is "your space you can customize as your own, where Hark sends timely information to you". Notifications have evolved into Action Buttons, which "solve themselves with a tap": you press the word "pay" to pay a bill or "cancel" to end an unused subscription — [Introducing Hark Pro](https://hark.com/articles/introducing-hark-pro)
- "Notifications give you problems. Action Buttons give you solutions." Action Buttons are generated cards offering actions like "Pay" or "Cancel", built from email and conversations. Each button press performs one action. Hark is "designed to think with you and sometimes ahead of you" — [hark.com/using-hark](https://hark.com/using-hark)
- Action Buttons are "suggested and prioritized based on what Hark knows you have to do. All you have to do is click on it and Hark will perform that action from start to finish" — [hark.com](https://hark.com)
- **Pacing** (company example): for a movie request, Hark finds seats, checks parking, offers to reserve a nearby garage spot, then sends the entry QR code closer to showtime. Steps are spread out "so they arrive at the right moment instead of all at once in chat" — [Introducing Hark Pro](https://hark.com/articles/introducing-hark-pro); Gizmodo notes it could not verify this — [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/hark-pro-ai-ui-2000822253)
- **Threshold trigger** (company example): Hark flags a child's school lunch card running low and suggests topping it up before the week ends. "If you allow it, Hark can even solve some of these problems for you" — [Introducing Hark Pro](https://hark.com/articles/introducing-hark-pro)
- Card types observed in the Gizmodo demo: a colleague awaiting approval, receipt uploads to Ramp, a delivery to authorize, flight check-ins and payments. The reviewer said the cards "are not revolutionary" and resemble Gemini, Siri, Samsung Galaxy AI and Poppy, though the presentation feels OS-like — [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/hark-pro-ai-ui-2000822253)
- App Store description: "looks ahead and offers to act before problems arise". Action Buttons are "one-tap actions from notifications, such as paying a bill or RSVPing". Email and Slack integration triage messages — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hark-pro/id6772362941)
- Version 2.4.0 (Oct 17) added a **Lock Screen widget** and "**subtle notifications when Projects need attention**" — [App Store version history](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hark-pro/id6772362941)
- **Scheduled Tasks** handle recurring requests, for example a daily coffee delivery or a weekly meeting reminder. You create them by asking Hark in chat. **Specialties** are multi-step workflows Hark memorizes for tasks you repeat; Hark "can set them up automatically or on request" — [hark.com/using-hark](https://hark.com/using-hark)
- Latency caveat from Hark's own docs: tracking deliveries may lag a few minutes, so other apps' push notifications may arrive before Hark's — [hark.com/using-hark](https://hark.com/using-hark). This implies Hark polls and watches external state such as order tracking.
- Panels refresh on their own: the Incrypted reviewer's Netflix and Rotten Tomatoes panel "updates automatically" with no need to repeat the request weekly — [Incrypted](https://incrypted.com/en/hark-pro-review-private-ai-assistant/). An early-access user had stock, weather, news and Strava panels updating on a regular schedule — [note.com user report, 2026-10-07](https://note.com/ad_motsu/n/n13450b227741?hl=en)
- Fast Company beta (secondhand): Hark spotted an oil change due in the reporter's email and handled much of the booking. The reporter still had to supply some details and pick a time — [juliangoldie.com, citing Fast Company](https://juliangoldie.com/hark-pro-review/)
- Analytics Insight says development details point to a home screen that gathers recurring tasks, connected services, "daily briefs" and items needing attention — [Analytics Insight](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/news/ai-startup-hark-debuts-proactive-assistant-as-hardware-plans-take-shape). No Hark primary source mentions a "daily brief", so treat this as unconfirmed.
- A competitor's blog (Carly/CalBot) says Action Buttons appear on Home unprompted after Hark reads your email, and that they wait for your tap — [usecarly.com, 2026-10-07](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/hark-ai/)
- Learning is implicit. The note.com user says Hark automatically learns "your preferences, schedule, and frequently mentioned people" without being told to remember — [note.com](https://note.com/ad_motsu/n/n13450b227741?hl=en)

### Inferences
- **Triggers seen in public material:** incoming email (bills, subscriptions, receipts, RSVPs, service reminders such as an oil change); calendar and travel (flight check-in); connected-account state (a lunch-card balance under a threshold, delivery status, approvals waiting in work tools such as Ramp and Slack); time (scheduled tasks; paced steps tied to an event time). **No location-based or spending-anomaly trigger** is described publicly. Spending appears as an on-demand "spending audit" task and as payment cards.
- **Surfaces, ranked by intrusiveness:** Home cards and Panels (pull) → Lock Screen widget (ambient) → "subtle" push notifications for Projects → paced follow-ups (QR code, parking). Chat is deliberately *not* the proactive channel ("instead of all at once in chat").
- **Opt-in model:** generating suggestions is on by default once you connect email or accounts. Executing them requires a tap (one tap = one action). Fully autonomous resolution needs prior permission per category. This is a three-step ladder: observe → suggest → act if pre-authorized.
- Ideas for Alma: (a) model proactive output as **actionable cards with one verb** ("Pay", "Cancel", "RSVP"), not alert text; (b) **pace** multi-step plans against event times; (c) make suggestions a pull surface (Home/widget), with push kept for "needs attention"; (d) let repeated workflows become named "Specialties" automatically; (e) let user-built mini-dashboards (Panels) refresh themselves.

### Gaps
- No published cadence, frequency caps, quiet hours, ranking logic or per-source toggles for Action Buttons. Not found in /using-hark, the privacy policy or press.
- No confirmed "daily brief" or "morning digest" feature.
- Unknown whether Hark ever starts a chat message by itself, as opposed to only cards and notifications.

## 3. Named features: how each works (Handoff, Action Buttons, Panels, Projects, Scheduled Tasks, email, spending, booking)

### Takeaway
**Handoff** is a per-request cloud VM (browser + file system + terminal) driven by Hark's own computer-use model. It runs up to 6 browsers in parallel, logs in with vaulted credentials, shows progress in a small watch window and can be cancelled. **Panels** are LLM-generated mini-apps on the Home screen that are fed by connectors and can be queried. Everything else (email sorting, spending review, booking, ordering) is delivered by chat, API connectors (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, MCP) or Handoff.

### Cited Findings
- **Handoff:** "For every request, Hark spins up a dedicated virtual computer with its own browser, file system, and terminal". Output is "a sequence of cursor and keyboard inputs that directly influence the state of a live browser". "You can also connect your existing accounts, and Handoff logs in safely on your behalf", using "your saved addresses, preferences, and history" — [Introducing Hark Handoff, 2026-08-05](https://hark.com/articles/introducing-hark-handoff)
- Handoff benchmark claims: "the top spot on the Online-Mind2Web human evaluation leaderboard". It "on average outperforms GPT 5.4 by 8 points and Opus 4.8 by 2 points". Hark claims "an order of magnitude lower cost per token than competing frontier models" — [Introducing Hark Handoff](https://hark.com/articles/introducing-hark-handoff). Incrypted notes that part of the benchmarking used Hark's own harness and LLM judge, with no independent verification — [Incrypted](https://incrypted.com/en/hark-pro-review-private-ai-assistant/)
- Handoff "uses up to 6 browsers at once" and can log in to "millions of websites". Outside the browser, Hark connects to Google and Outlook via APIs, reads files, accesses databases and uses "an open source MCP standard" — [Introducing Hark Pro](https://hark.com/articles/introducing-hark-pro)
- Handoff has "its own secure computer with a full browser that's individual to you". Listed uses: building websites, research, placing orders, making slides — [hark.com](https://hark.com)
- Start Handoff by asking for anything that needs a web browser. It can sign in, buy items and fill out forms — [hark.com/using-hark](https://hark.com/using-hark)
- You watch the task in a small window and can cancel at any point — [usecarly.com](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/hark-ai/); "You can watch our CUA perform actions as they work on your behalf", "There's always a button to cancel your order" — [hark.com/security](https://hark.com/security)
- Handoff speed: in a demo, a DoorDash Filet-O-Fish order took several minutes, then asked the user only to confirm payment. The reviewer could have ordered faster in the DoorDash app. Chowdhury argues agents pay off on complex tasks, such as finding a dentist that matches insurance, rating and availability — [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/hark-pro-ai-ui-2000822253)
- **Panels:** "Custom mini-apps you create by describing what you want". You can ask them questions and connect services like Strava, Spotify and Venmo — [hark.com](https://hark.com). They live in Home, and tapping "Reply" on a Panel lets you redesign it or explore its data — [hark.com/using-hark](https://hark.com/using-hark). Examples include Strava workouts, Netflix releases with Rotten Tomatoes scores, and ParentSquare grades — [Introducing Hark Pro](https://hark.com/articles/introducing-hark-pro)
- **Projects:** "dedicated threads for longer term work", one topic each, with related files kept together — [hark.com/using-hark](https://hark.com/using-hark)
- **Chat:** there is one main continuous thread with no "New Chat". A user found it hard to track several parallel requests this way — [note.com](https://note.com/ad_motsu/n/n13450b227741?hl=en)
- **Email:** "Any good assistant needs your email". Hark uses it for "a snapshot of your life", scheduling and sending messages. Direct API integrations include Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 — [hark.com/using-hark](https://hark.com/using-hark). Multiple Google accounts can be linked — [note.com](https://note.com/ad_motsu/n/n13450b227741?hl=en)
- Example tasks on the site: flight and hotel booking, food ordering, insurance-quote comparison, dentist booking, dinner reservations, email sorting, spending audits, and "submit expenses from email receipts" — [hark.com](https://hark.com)
- Messages Hark sends are treated as yours. Hark identifies itself as an AI when a recipient is likely to read them — [usecarly.com](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/hark-ai/) (secondary; not confirmed on a Hark page)
- A user reports Hark handled a complex SBA loan payment and filled a Google Sheet — [App Store review "Ol' guy", Oct 21](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hark-pro/id6772362941)
- Not supported: phone calls and SMS. Adcock: "We'll get there." Examples are almost all US services (DoorDash, Target, Walmart, Resy, Venmo) — [Incrypted](https://incrypted.com/en/hark-pro-review-private-ai-assistant/)

### Inferences
- Combining an API path (Gmail, Outlook, Slack, MCP) for reading with a browser path (Handoff) for acting on sites that have no API is the core architecture. Alma's local tools, Chrome MCP and channels map onto the same split.
- "Specialties" (auto-memorized workflows) mean Hark stores procedures as well as facts. That is a procedural-memory idea worth copying.

### Gaps
- There is no public detail on whether a user can take manual control of the Handoff browser mid-task (for example, to solve a CAPTCHA). The "secure input form" for credentials suggests sensitive fields are entered outside the agent.
- No mechanism is published for "spending review" beyond it being a task example and payment and transaction data being collected.

## 4. Approvals, encryption claims, memory

### Takeaway
Hark's default is **confirm before spending or sending**, and users can opt to let it act without confirmation. Its encryption story is inconsistent across pages. The homepage says "end-to-end encryption" and the launch post says "Even we can't see inside Secured by Hark". The security page and privacy policy, however, only claim an encrypted vault for credentials and cards, tokenization, encryption in transfer between countries, and zero-retention third-party models. Memory stores "facts, preferences, and summaries" indefinitely until deleted. It can be reviewed, edited, deleted or turned off, and is unavailable in the EEA, UK and Switzerland.

### Cited Findings
- Hark "checks in with you before doing something that requires your approval" — [hark.com](https://hark.com)
- The privacy policy says that before acting on your behalf "it will ask you to confirm, unless you have chosen to let it act without confirmation" — [hark.com/privacy-policy, last updated 2026-11-05](https://hark.com/privacy-policy)
- "We start tasks by defaulting to consent"; "There's always a button to cancel your order" — [hark.com/security](https://hark.com/security). The wording is ambiguous: it could mean "we assume consent to start".
- Users report "approval button appears for purchases" — [note.com](https://note.com/ad_motsu/n/n13450b227741?hl=en). Another: Hark "always checks with me before spending money or sending anything" — [App Store review, Oct 24](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hark-pro/id6772362941). By default it asks before it sends any email or spends money — [usecarly.com](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/hark-ai/)
- Encryption claims, in order of strength:
  - Homepage: "Secured by hark" has end-to-end encryption, "a vault that absolutely no person has access to" — [hark.com](https://hark.com)
  - Launch post: an in-house encrypted vault for credit cards and passwords; "Even we can't see inside Secured by Hark"; passwords stay encrypted from the vault to the website — [Introducing Hark Pro](https://hark.com/articles/introducing-hark-pro)
  - Security page: "guard your data like a bank", "We never see your most sensitive data", "Hark stores your most sensitive data in a vault", "Passwords, credit card numbers, etc, are tokenized in our hands", "Models used from vetted third parties have 0 data retention", Hark "prioritizes its own models whenever best" — [hark.com/security](https://hark.com/security)
  - Privacy policy: "We encrypt Personal Data when we transfer it between countries"; connector access tokens are encrypted; sign-in details are kept in "Hark Secure, our encrypted store". Third-party AI providers are "contractually prohibited from using it for any other purpose, including to train their models" — [hark.com/privacy-policy](https://hark.com/privacy-policy)
  - "Secured by Hark" has not been independently audited, and the base model is undisclosed — [Incrypted](https://incrypted.com/en/hark-pro-review-private-ai-assistant/)
- Data Hark collects (App Store privacy labels, linked to identity): financial info, precise and coarse location, contacts, emails or texts, photos and videos, identifiers, usage, diagnostics — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hark-pro/id6772362941). Precise location is collected only if allowed in device settings. Apple Health data can be read if connected — [privacy policy](https://hark.com/privacy-policy)
- **Memory:** stores "facts, preferences, and summaries". "We keep your Memories until you delete them or your account". Deleted items are removed from active systems within 30 days and from backups within 90 days. You review, edit or delete them by asking Hark or in Privacy Settings, and "you can turn Memory off there at any time". Memory isn't available in the EEA, UK or Switzerland — [privacy policy](https://hark.com/privacy-policy)
- "Just ask Hark to forget a memory and it will". Hark "learns from your conversations, and it applies that context to everything you ask it" — [hark.com/using-hark](https://hark.com/using-hark). Example remembered facts: no mayo, window seats, pugs, document formatting — [Introducing Hark Pro](https://hark.com/articles/introducing-hark-pro)
- Hark will "never sell your data or serve you ads" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hark-pro/id6772362941)

### Inferences
- The "end-to-end" claim most plausibly covers only the credential and card vault, which is tokenized and injected into Handoff logins. It cannot cover general chat and memory, which servers and third-party models must read in plaintext to work. Treat "E2E" as marketing overreach.
- Approval model for Alma: by default, actions are gated by **category** (spend money, send messages), with a per-category opt-out ("act without confirmation"), plus a visible live view and an always-available cancel. Alma's `publish` ToolRisk already matches this. The new pieces are the watch window and the per-category opt-out.
- Memory UX: conversational forgetting ("forget X") plus a settings list to review and edit, and a global off switch.

### Gaps
- There is no public description of the Privacy Settings memory UI, such as whether memories are listed or grouped, or whether they carry sources.
- The meaning of "defaulting to consent" is unclear.
- No technical whitepaper on the vault (key custody, HSM, client-side keys) was found.

## 5. Reviews and user reports: praise and complaints, especially about proactivity

### Takeaway
Early sentiment (October 2026) is positive on polish, terseness and "gets things done". The complaints concern **speed** (Handoff slower than doing it yourself), **opaque and low usage caps**, bugs and crashes, a single-thread chat, no calls or SMS, and US-centric coverage. I found **no user complaints that proactivity is annoying**. Gizmodo instead said the cards are unoriginal and the anticipatory claims unverified. No Reddit or Product Hunt discussion was found.

### Cited Findings
- App Store rating: 4.7/5 (2.8K ratings). Reviews praise email, grocery shopping, bill paying, budgeting, short replies, Home Panels, scheduled reminders and credential handling. One says Hark is "not cute or sycophantic". One review ("THE PRO!!!") appears AI-generated — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hark-pro/id6772362941)
- Gizmodo: the demo task was slower than doing it manually; the anticipatory claims are unverified; the cards resemble Gemini, Siri, Galaxy AI and Poppy — [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/hark-pro-ai-ui-2000822253)
- Early-user complaints, collected by a competitor's blog: the free cap lasted about 10 minutes for one user; there is no way to see remaining usage; the GitHub connector was broken on day one; "quite a lot of bugs"; browsers crashing; "slowest personal ai agent". Chowdhury conceded it "might not be faster than Muse" — [usecarly.com (competitor, 2026-10-07)](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/hark-ai/)
- User report (Japan): pressing Enter on PC sends mid-IME conversion, so the user stopped using PC; one chat makes several requests hard to track. The free tier is valuable "even with limitations" — [note.com, 2026-10-07](https://note.com/ad_motsu/n/n13450b227741?hl=en)
- Reviewer: polished and genuinely proactive, but can be slow, can't make calls yet, and needs deep account access — [juliangoldie.com](https://juliangoldie.com/hark-pro-review/) (single-author SEO blog)
- A marketing guide says Hark paces recommendations instead of flooding chat with notifications — [layer3labs.io](https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/hark-pro-for-marketing) (vendor-adjacent)

### Inferences
- The absence of "annoying" complaints probably comes from the design choice to put proactivity in a pull surface (Home cards) rather than push. It may also just mean the product is only a month old.
- Speed and quota transparency are the main friction points. For Alma, local execution plus visible budgets (already in Alma's company budgets) answer both.

### Gaps
- No Reddit, Product Hunt or Google Play review text was retrieved. No X founder posts on proactivity were found.
- The original Fast Company review text could not be read.

## 6. Competitors it is compared to

### Takeaway
Hark Pro is most often compared to **OpenAI Dots** (ChatGPT Pro, cloud computer with a browser), **Meta Muse** (personal agent, September 2026) and **Instinct** (invite-only, iMessage, WhatsApp and phone). Gizmodo also compares its proactive cards to Gemini, Siri, Samsung Galaxy AI and Poppy.

### Cited Findings
- Dots (OpenAI) runs on "GPT-6 Astra" in ChatGPT, Slack and Microsoft Teams, from $100/month with no free tier. It is the closest design match, since both use a cloud computer with a browser. Muse (Meta) launched in September 2026 — [Incrypted](https://incrypted.com/en/hark-pro-review-private-ai-assistant/)
- Muse has the same prices as Hark, more built-in connectors, and runs on WhatsApp and Mac, but opts users into AI training by default. Instinct is a free invite-only beta that works through iMessage, WhatsApp and phone. Hark's differentiators are Panels, the visible browser window and no-training — [usecarly.com](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/hark-ai/)
- The cards resemble Gemini, Siri, Samsung Galaxy AI and Poppy — [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/hark-pro-ai-ui-2000822253)

### Inferences
- Hark differs from Dots and Muse mostly in UX: a GUI-first design with Home, Action Buttons, Panels and pacing, rather than raw agent capability. This is where Alma can borrow most.

### Gaps
- Dots, Muse and Instinct were not independently verified here. They are cited only as reported by the sources above.

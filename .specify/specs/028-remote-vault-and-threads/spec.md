## 1. Summary
From the phone app of the remote access feature (spec 026), the person can (1) browse and read, read-only, the notes of their active knowledge vault, narrowed by a list of folders they exclude on the PC, and filter them by name; (2) list their other conversations and read them, and, with a further switch on, continue them by sending messages into them (company conversations excepted), which makes the PC run that conversation's agent for the phone; and (3) use an app that feels finished: clear navigation, a calm loading, empty and error state on every screen, and the same Spanish and English as the rest of Alma. Notes and History are two separate switches (scopes) on the PC, and continuing conversations is a sub-switch under History; each is off at every turn-on and each is confirmed in a system dialog the page cannot click. The phone keeps no note and no conversation of its own. Nothing here writes to the vault, and nothing new goes to an Alma server or a push service.

## 2. Problem and goal
Today the phone app reaches only the chat with one shared remote thread, approvals of that thread, the house and the cameras (026). The owner's notes and the rest of their conversations are invisible from the phone, so away from the PC they must ask the agent to fetch a note in chat, or wait, and cannot pick up a conversation they started on the PC. The phone app itself is a first cut: four tabs, a Settings screen with one button, and screens whose empty, loading and error states were not designed as a set.

Facts the design respects (verified in code and specs):
- The remote-chat agent can already call `read_note` and `search_knowledge` (risk `read`, no approval) and quote notes into the phone chat (009 BR-15, 026 §3 Out). This spec adds a direct reader and leaves that path as it is (BR-27).
- A thread's title is the first line of its first message (002 BR-2): a list of titles is already content.
- 026 BR-32: the phone keeps only its key and the app shell, and the service worker never caches `/api/` responses.
- A note can be up to 5 MiB (008 BR-4); the request/response body cap is 1 MB and the photo cap 2 MB (026 BR-31).
- Writing into a conversation means the PC runs that conversation's agent: with its tools, its working folder and its provider, under the approval policy, one run per thread at a time (002 BR-3).
- The approval mode ("Permitir todo", "Solo lectura", the ask-per-risk modes) is one global setting (002 BR-13); the phone cannot change it.

Done means: with the switches on, the owner can walk their vault and read a note, and see a list of conversations, read one and, if they turned that on, continue it, from the phone, with every refusal explained by a concrete cause; with the switches off, or the phone revoked, nothing of the vault or the conversations is reachable and nothing is left on the phone; a run the phone starts never ends up stuck with no way to see why; and every screen of the app behaves predictably when it is loading, empty, failing or offline.

## 3. Scope
**In:**
- A read-only note reader on the phone: list a folder, open a note, find by name (not by content), show images and follow links inside a note. Reachable notes are the whole active vault minus the folders the person excluded on the PC.
- A conversation browser on the phone: list conversations (everything the PC's Chat tab lists), open one to read, and, with the continue-conversations sub-switch on, continue it by sending messages into it by its id, and stop a run the phone started. The Chat tab keeps the shared remote thread as in 026.
- Two new scopes on the PC, **Notes** and **History**, and under History the sub-switch **continue conversations**; their switches in the "Remote access" tab (specs 006/026), their native confirmations, the exclusion list, and the narrow-API operations that serve them, inside the same remote security layer (authorization, session, limits, audit).
- Showing, and letting the phone answer, the approvals of a run the phone started (BR-20), and the content-free push for them.
- Audit and remote-activity visibility of note opens, image fetches, conversation opens, sends and stops (BR-30).
- What the phone stores (nothing) and what happens to an open note or conversation when a scope is turned off, the phone is revoked, or the thread or note disappears.
- Phone app polish as user-visible behavior: navigation, screen organization, loading/empty/error/offline states for every screen, Settings content, i18n es/en parity, the touch look (`umbra-ui` adapted, 026 BR-17), the accessibility bar.
- Docs in the same PR as the implementation: `docs/security/RISKS.md` (R-14), `CONTROLS.md`, `PRIVACY.md`, the glossary, and the specs of §7.1.

**Out (and why):**
- Writing to the vault from the phone (create, edit, rename, move, trash a note or folder): the owner asked for the notes to be read-only to start; a later spec, its own scope.
- Content search or semantic search of notes from the phone: the phone browses, reads and filters by name only (BR-15).
- Continuing company conversations from the phone, and listing them (BR-16, BR-31).
- Company vaults and any vault other than the active one (BR-22).
- Renaming, deleting or changing the agent, model or folder of a conversation, and starting a new conversation with a chosen agent, from the phone (BR-32).
- Making the phone's approval rules stricter than the app's normal policy (BR-21).
- Any sound, music or light theme on the phone; any language switch inside the app (BR-36).
- "Ask Alma about this note" (attaching a note to a chat) from the phone (BR-27).
- Offline notes or conversations: the phone stores none (BR-26).
- The chat-disappearing bug on the phone: a separate /fix. This spec assumes it has landed and adds no criterion about it.
- The "Hey Alma" voice feature on the phone: the owner asked about it but decided nothing. It is recorded only as a possible later spec (029 or later); nothing in this spec prepares it (BR-37).
- The relay (spec 027+), live camera video, a native phone app, Alma accounts: as in 026.
- Changing how the desktop vault, the knowledge index or the thread store work (008, 009, 002): reused.
- Exporting or erasing notes or conversations differently: unchanged (BR-31).

## 4. Users and stories
- **US-1** As a person away from the PC, I want to browse my vault and read a note on the phone, so that I can consult what I wrote without waking the PC chat or asking the agent.
- **US-2** As a person, I want to find a note by name from the phone, so that browsing folders is not the only way. Content search is not part of this spec.
- **US-3** As a person, I want to see my other conversations and read one from the phone, and, if I turned it on, continue it by sending a message into it, so that I can pick up what I discussed on the PC or in Telegram without going to the PC.
- **US-4** As a person, I want Notes and History to be two separate switches on the PC and continuing conversations a further sub-switch, each off every time remote access is turned on and each confirmed in a system dialog, a way to exclude folders from the phone, and the phone to keep nothing of my notes or conversations, so that a stolen or lost phone and a stranger on my tailnet get as little as possible, and so that I can let a phone read my history without letting it act in it.
- **US-5** As a person or reviewer, I want these reads and writes on the record and visible in the remote-activity list on the PC, so that I can tell what a phone looked at and what it made Alma do.
- **US-6** As a person, I want the phone app to feel finished: obvious navigation, a clear loading, empty and error state on every screen, a Settings screen that tells me what this phone can do, and my language, so that it does not feel like a beta.
- **US-7** As the maintainer, I want the new reads and writes to go through the same remote security layer, the same vault code, the same thread store and the same run and approval path as the desktop (never a second, weaker path), and the security docs updated in the same PR, so that the attack surface stays described and testable.
- **US-8** As a person, I want to answer on the phone the approvals of a run I started from the phone in a conversation, when I allowed the phone to approve, and to be nudged when one is waiting, so that the run does not stall while I am away.

## 6. Business rules
Rules the code must obey. Rules marked (human) are answers the human gave on 2026-11-08, either one by one or by accepting the planner's suggestion as written; the others are the owner's request or an existing written rule applied as a floor.

- **BR-1** (owner request, human OQ-17) Notes are read-only: no operation creates, writes, renames, moves, trashes or deletes a note or folder. Conversations are not read-only: the phone may read them and, with the sub-switch of BR-18 on, continue them by sending messages (BR-16) and stop a run it started (BR-32).
- **BR-2** (026 BR-1, BR-7, BR-14) The new operations are part of the same remote channel: closed list of operations, a strict schema each, session authentication, the pure authorization function, and no input from the transport. Network position never authenticates.
- **BR-3** (floor) No path is reachable from the phone that the desktop vault would refuse (hidden segments, outside the vault, a symlink out, `node_modules`: 008 BR-3, AC-11.1, AC-11.2; 022 AC-4.6).
- **BR-4** (floor, 026 BR-25; human OQ-38) A conversation is shown on the phone only as the text of user and assistant messages, the running flag, approval cards (BR-20) and the run's failure cause. Tool names, tool inputs, tool results, thinking and attachments never leave the PC through the read operations, apart from what an approval card already shows.
- **BR-5** (026 BR-11) The settings of the new scopes, the sub-switch and the exclusion list are changed only on the PC, through `main` and its native confirmations; the coordinator's `settings.patch` cannot touch them and no tool exists for them (this spec adds no tool and no `ToolRisk`).
- **BR-6** (026 BR-29) Widening what a phone can reach needs a native confirmation in `main`; narrowing needs none. Scopes are global, never per device.
- **BR-7** (026 BR-3, BR-27) This spec adds no Alma server and no new third party. No push carries note or conversation content.
- **BR-8** (026 BR-15, BR-30) Secrets never appear in logs, audit entries, errors or fixtures; the remote audit module and the `audit()` contract are not changed.
- **BR-9** (026 BR-20, spec 025) UI text is by dictionary key; the phone's keys live in the PWA's own es/en dictionary and the PC's in the shared dictionaries, each with a parity test. English is US English and Spanish rioplatense.
- **BR-10** (026 BR-17) UI work on the PC tab and on the phone loads the `console-ui` and `umbra-ui` skills at the plan and implement stages; the plan names the components and tokens used and invents none.
- **BR-11** (026 BR-31) The existing limits apply to the new operations (1 MB body, 2 MB picture, 60 requests per minute per device, 2 held long polls per device, 30-second timeout, 8000-character message). A change to a limit is a spec change.
- **BR-12** Reading is not a tool call: the read operations are not `read_note` or `search_knowledge` and are not subject to the approval policy of tools; they are gated only by the scope switches and the remote security layer. Sending a message is different: it starts a run, whose own tool calls follow their own `ToolRisk` and approvals (026 BR-13, BR-21 here).
- **BR-13** (human OQ-1, OQ-15) **Notes** and **History** are two separate scopes. Each has its own switch in the "Remote access" tab, is global, is off every time remote access is turned on, and needs its own native confirmation to be turned on.
- **BR-14** (human OQ-3) Reachable notes are the whole active vault minus the folders in an exclusion list built on the PC. Excluding a folder needs no confirmation (it narrows access). There is no allow-list. The list is kept across turn-ons of remote access.
- **BR-15** (human OQ-6) The Notes scope allows browsing, reading and filtering by name. It does not allow content search or semantic search; `vault.search` is not exposed to the phone.
- **BR-16** (human OQ-17) The History scope lets the phone read and continue conversations other than company conversations. Company conversations cannot be continued from the phone.
- **BR-17** (floor, 002 BR-3, 026 BR-12) A message sent from the phone is processed by the PC like a message typed on the PC: the same run path, the same approval broker, the same abort path, one run per conversation at a time.
- **BR-18** (human OQ-28) Continuing conversations is a sub-switch under History ("continue conversations"): off every time remote access is turned on, with its own native confirmation to be turned on. With History on and the sub-switch off, the phone can list and read conversations but cannot send into them (read-only History is possible). The Chat tab's shared remote thread is governed by the chat scope, not by this sub-switch.
- **BR-19** (human OQ-27) The Chat tab keeps the shared remote thread and "New conversation" exactly as in 026. Opening a conversation from the list and sending writes into that conversation by its id; this never re-points the shared remote thread.
- **BR-20** (human OQ-18, OQ-41) The approvals of a run a device started in a conversation by id are visible and answerable on that device, only when the approve scope is on: each answer is valid once, "Siempre permitir" is never offered, and `publish` always asks (026 BR-12). Only the device that started the run sees and answers its approvals; a run started on the PC or on another surface is never the phone's, and approvals of runs the device did not start in a conversation remain invisible to it. The shared remote thread keeps the rule of 026 (AC-5.6, AC-5.25): its pending approvals are visible to a device with the approve scope on. With the approve scope off, the phone shows that the run is waiting for an approval on the PC (`remote.pwa.thread.waiting`) and the approval stays pending for the other surfaces.
- **BR-21** (human OQ-32) A run the phone starts in a conversation follows the app's normal approval policy, as the shared remote thread does today: each tool keeps its `ToolRisk`, the approval mode decides as on the PC, `publish` always asks. Nothing stricter is forced on phone-started runs. R-14 and `CONTROLS.md` state that in "Permitir todo" mode `exec` does not ask, a phone-started run included.
- **BR-22** (human OQ-2, OQ-4, OQ-5) Only the active vault is served, at the moment of each request; company vaults and the other vaults of the list are never reachable. The native confirmation names the vault folder. `Alma/` (`Recuerdos.md`, `Conocimientos/`) has no special rule: reachable like any note, excludable like any folder. Confinement is the desktop's (BR-3), and one identical answer (`remote.pwa.note.unavailable`) covers missing, hidden, excluded and outside-the-vault paths, so existence is not revealed.

## 7. Constraints and risks
- **Layers touched** (`docs/architecture.md`): `shared` (two new members of `RemoteScope`, the sub-switch and the exclusion list in the settings type and default, `planRemoteChange`, the closed route table and its schemas, the pure refusal-to-key mapping, the status shape, i18n keys); `coordinator/channels/remote` (new handlers for notes and conversations, wired through injected dependencies to the active vault, the thread store and the existing run, abort and approval entry points, never to a file API or a copy of the run loop of their own; the approvals list and the push gain the runs a device started; a run is tied to the device that started it); `coordinator/index.ts` (wiring only); `main` (settings keys, the new native confirmations); `renderer` (the "Remote access" tab gets the two switches, the sub-switch and the exclusion list with its stale marker); `remote-client` (Notes and Conversations screens, navigation, states, Settings, dictionaries); docs. How the exclusion list picks folders (a view of the vault tree) is a plan matter.
- **ToolRisk:** no new tool, so no new `ToolRisk` (BR-5). The read operations are not tool calls (BR-12). A message sent into a conversation starts a run in which every tool keeps its own risk under the app's normal policy (BR-21).
- **Security / privacy:**
  - New data flow: vault content and conversation text now leave the PC to the owner's own paired phone over Tailscale. Until now vault content left the PC only toward a model provider (008 §7, 022 BR-9). `docs/security/PRIVACY.md`, `CONTROLS.md` and R-14 (remote attack surface, 026) are updated in the same PR as the implementation; 022 BR-9 and BR-12 are amended (§7.1).
  - Attack surface: a stolen, unlocked, paired phone with History and the continue sub-switch on can make Alma run an agent inside a desktop conversation, with that conversation's working folder and tools, not only inside the remote thread. The controls are the History switch, the continue sub-switch, the usual approval policy (`publish` always asks), the approve scope, the 15-day idle expiry, revoke, the tray kill switch (which aborts phone-started runs: BR-35), and the one-run-per-device limit (BR-33). Because the policy is not stricter for the phone (BR-21), in "Permitir todo" mode `exec` and `write` run without asking; R-14 and `CONTROLS.md` say so plainly.
  - Alma cannot stop the phone from copying, sharing or screenshotting text it displays; this is accepted, not mitigated, and the docs should say so.
  - A note or conversation is data, not instruction: opening it for reading never gives it to a model (BR-27). A message sent into a conversation does go to that conversation's model provider, as any message does.
  - Cost: a phone-started run spends the same provider tokens and budgets as a PC-started one; the only cap is one phone-started run at a time per device (BR-33).
  - The status operation tells the phone which scopes are on (AC-6.11); it is the owner's own phone and the phone learns this from refusals anyway.
- **Performance / platform:** Windows paths are vault-relative with `/`, case-insensitive for any name list including the exclusion list (008 BR-2); notes may contain CRLF and are UTF-8 (008 BR-17). A vault can hold thousands of entries and a note up to 5 MiB while the response cap is 1 MB: whether the folder list is returned folder by folder, how the whole-vault name filter is computed (up to 50 results), and how the run-to-device tie is held in memory are plan matters. A note with many images could trip the 60 requests per minute limit (BR-23); the plan must not raise the limit.
- **Dependencies:** 026 implemented far enough to have the route table, the scope settings and the PWA shell; the separate chat-disappearing /fix should land first so the chat screen under test is stable.

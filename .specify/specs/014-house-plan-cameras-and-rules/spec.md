# 014 — Casa: plano, cámaras y reglas

## 1. Summary
The intelligent half of "Casa". Alma keeps its own 2D, top-down floor plan of the home (floors, rooms, doors, devices placed on it, lights that bathe their room in their own colour). The plan can be built from an Excalidraw drawing, from a photo or sketch read by a vision model, or by hand, and edited by dragging in the window or by asking an agent. Agents can look through the house's cameras (one still, a still sent to Telegram, or a time-limited watch), look at any picture the person sends, activate and create modes (Home Assistant scenes), and create rules: simple ones run inside Home Assistant as automations and cost no AI; rules that need judgment wake an agent with the event so it can look and decide.

## 2. Problem and goal
A list of devices does not say where anything is, and a rule such as "if the patio camera sees movement at night, tell me only if it is a person" cannot be written as a plain automation. The goal is a plan the person recognises as their house, a way to see through cameras on demand or for a bounded time, and two kinds of rules whose cost is predictable: the ones that never spend tokens, and the ones that spend them only when they fire.

## 3. Scope
**In:**
- The floor plan: data model, persistence (`plan.json`), validation, operations, the plan view of the Casa window, edit mode (drag devices, rooms and corners; floors; "Sin ubicar" tray).
- Building the plan from an Excalidraw drawing, from an image (vision), and by hand; replacing a floor's shape.
- Tools: `home_plan`, `home_plan_edit`, `home_plan_from_drawing`, `home_plan_from_image`, `look_at_image`, `camera_look`, `camera_photo`, `camera_watch`, `home_scene`, `home_modes`, `home_rules`.
- Vision: which model looks, how pictures are loaded (`loadPicture`, roots, size, types), how pictures reach an agent (as text, never in the conversation).
- Camera stills (`cameraSnapshot`) and the live picture of a camera in the device panel.
- Modes (scenes) and the "Modos" buttons.
- Rules: Home Assistant automations created by `home_rules`, judgment rules and camera watches (`src/coordinator/home/triggers.ts`), how a state change wakes an agent, cooldown and time window, what the woken agent may do, the "Reglas" tab.

**Out (and why):**
- The Home Assistant engine (WSL distro, install, start, health), the connection and reconnection of the hub, the device model (`HomeThing`, kinds, `home_overview`/`home_find`), plain device control (`home_control`, `home_secure_control`, `home_call_service`), adding/renaming/removing devices and config flows, device history (`home_history`), the device panel controls other than the camera picture, and Configuración → Casa: all in [../013-house-engine-and-devices/spec.md](../013-house-engine-and-devices/spec.md). This spec only says how the plan and the rules *use* the hub (`snapshot`, `onChange`, `cameraSnapshot`, `ensureArea`, `saveScene`, `saveAutomation`).
- The approval policy itself (modes, "Siempre permitir", auto-review, how `home` and `write` are treated): [../004-approvals/spec.md](../004-approvals/spec.md). This spec states each tool's `ToolRisk` and the consequence.
- Telegram as a channel (sending, receiving photos, authorised users): [../010-telegram/spec.md](../010-telegram/spec.md). Reminders/routines (a different, time-based wake-up): [../018-routines-and-reminders/spec.md](../018-routines-and-reminders/spec.md). The vault and `.excalidraw` files in it: [../008-vault/spec.md](../008-vault/spec.md), [../021-office-documents-and-drawings/spec.md](../021-office-documents-and-drawings/spec.md). Pictures generally (generation, posting): [../020-images/spec.md](../020-images/spec.md). Providers: [../005-providers/spec.md](../005-providers/spec.md). Erasing the inbox (`erasePersonalFiles`): [../022-security-and-privacy/spec.md](../022-security-and-privacy/spec.md).
- Audio (speakers, microphones, `home_speak`): not built.

## 4. Users and stories
- **US-1** As a person, I want to see my house as a plan where lit lights glow and I can tap a device, so that I know what is on at a glance.
- **US-2** As a person, I want Alma to build the plan from my Excalidraw drawing or a photo/sketch, or to draw it by hand, so that I do not have to measure anything.
- **US-3** As a person, I want to correct the plan by dragging (devices, rooms, corners), by adding floors and rooms, or by telling Alma, so that it matches my house.
- **US-4** As an agent, I want to read the plan (rooms with size and position, devices in each), so that I understand "la luz de al lado de la puerta" or "las de arriba".
- **US-5** As a person, I want to send Alma a picture (a plan, a room, a screenshot) and get an answer about it, so that it can help without me describing it.
- **US-6** As a person, I want to ask what a camera sees, have a still sent to my phone, or have a camera watched for a limited time, so that I stay informed without opening an app.
- **US-7** As a person, I want modes ("Noche", "Cine") I can create by chat and activate with a button or by asking, so that one action sets several devices.
- **US-8** As a person, I want simple rules ("a las 23 apagá todo") to run by themselves at no AI cost.
- **US-9** As a person, I want rules where Alma wakes up when something happens, looks and decides, with a time window and a cooldown, so that I am only bothered when it matters.
- **US-10** As the maintainer, I want the plan and the rules to survive restarts and corrupt files, and every edit (window or agent) to leave a valid plan.

## 5. Acceptance criteria

### The plan: data and operations (US-10, US-3, US-4)
- **AC-10.1** (US-10) Given a plan with malformed parts, when it is loaded or saved (`sanitizePlan`), then rooms with fewer than 3 valid points are dropped, empty names become "Habitación n" / "Piso n", duplicate item refs and non-finite coordinates are dropped, coordinates are rounded, and the plan always has at least one floor. Test: tests/home.test.ts › "sanitizePlan arregla lo roto y siempre deja un piso"
- **AC-10.2** (US-10) Given a floor with duplicated room, door or floor ids, when sanitized, then ids are made unique (a new `room-xxxxxx` style id replaces the repeat). [gap]
- **AC-10.3** (US-10) Given a saved plan, when the app starts again, then the same floors and rooms are loaded from `home/plan.json`. Test: tests/home.test.ts › "persiste entre arranques"
- **AC-10.4** (US-10) Given `plan.json` is missing, unreadable or not valid JSON, when the store opens, then the plan is the empty plan (one floor "Planta baja", id `piso-1`, no rooms) and the app does not fail. [gap]
- **AC-10.5** (US-10) Given a save, then the file is written to `plan.json.tmp` and renamed over `plan.json`. [gap]
- **AC-3.1** (US-3) Given a plan, when operations add, rename, place and remove rooms are applied, then rooms are matched by id or by name ignoring case and accents (exact first, then "contains"), placing a device again moves it instead of duplicating it, a device can be named by HA device id or entity id, and removing a room removes the devices that were only inside it. Test: tests/home.test.ts › "agrega, renombra, ubica y quita"
- **AC-3.2** (US-3) Given invalid operations, when applied, then a `PlanError` with a clear English message (spec 025 BR-17; the Spanish quotes below are the previous wording) is raised: a room name already used on that floor, a room without `rect` or `polygon` ("Falta la forma…"), an unknown room ("No existe la habitación…"), removing the last floor ("Tiene que quedar al menos un piso"). Test: tests/home.test.ts › "rechaza lo inválido con mensajes claros"
- **AC-3.3** (US-3) Given an unknown floor name, when an operation names it, then the error lists the existing floors; a floor is matched by id, by name (folded) or by "contains". [gap]
- **AC-3.4** (US-3) Given `place` with neither `room` nor both `x` and `y`, then it fails with "Indicá la habitación (room) o las coordenadas (x, y)."; given `room`, the device goes to a free spot near the room's centre, spread so several devices do not pile up and always inside the room. Test: tests/home.test.ts › "spotIn reparte varios dispositivos sin amontonarlos"
- **AC-3.5** (US-3) Given `add_door`, then a door segment `a`→`b` (cm, no validation against walls) is added to the floor; `add_floor`, `rename_floor`, `remove_floor`, `unplace`, `set_room_shape`, `rename_room` behave as their names say. [gap]
- **AC-3.6** (US-3) Given geometry helpers, then centroid and point-in-polygon work for rectangles. Test: tests/home.test.ts › "centroide y punto en polígono"
- **AC-3.7** (US-3) Given a floor's shape is replaced (drawing or photo), then the rooms and doors of that floor are replaced (existing doors are removed), a new room keeps the Home Assistant area of a previous room with the same name (ignoring case), devices no longer inside any room leave the plan, and the plan's `source` records where it came from. Test: tests/home.test.ts › "reemplazar las habitaciones conserva el área de HA de las que siguen con el mismo nombre"
- **AC-3.8** (US-3) Given a replaced floor, then devices still inside some room keep their position. [gap]
- **AC-3.9** (US-3) Given the hub publishes a snapshot, when a device has a Home Assistant area equal to the `areaId` of a room and is not on the plan, then it is put in that room (and `home.plan` is emitted); devices without a room/area are left in "Sin ubicar"; when nothing changes the same plan object is returned. Test: tests/home.test.ts › "ubica solo los dispositivos que ya tienen habitación"
- **AC-4.1** (US-4) Given a plan and devices, when `describePlan` runs, then for each floor it lists "Piso «name» (id …) — unidades en cm", each room with its box (`desde (x, y) w×h cm`) and area, the devices inside with position, devices outside any room, doors, and "Sin ubicar en el plano: …"; an empty floor says "(sin habitaciones: todavía no se dibujó el plano)". Test: tests/home.test.ts › "agrega, renombra, ubica y quita" 

### Saving the plan and Home Assistant areas (US-3, US-10)
- **AC-3.10** (US-3) Given the hub is connected, when a plan is saved (window or tool), then every room gets a Home Assistant area (found by name or created), a room whose name differs from its area's name renames the area, a stale `areaId` is dropped, and a device whose position changed into a room with an area is moved to that area; failures creating/renaming an area or moving a device are swallowed (the plan is still saved). [gap]
- **AC-3.11** (US-3) Given the hub is not connected, when a plan is saved, then it is saved without touching Home Assistant (rooms keep `areaId: null` until a later save). [gap]
- **AC-3.12** (US-3) Given a plan was saved, then a `home.plan` event is sent to the window and the saved (sanitized) plan is returned. [gap]
- **AC-3.13** (US-3) Given a room is removed from the plan, then its Home Assistant area is **not** deleted. [gap]

### Plan from a drawing (US-2)
- **AC-2.1** (US-2) Given an Excalidraw drawing, when converted, then every rectangle (or diamond, or closed line) is a room named by its bound text or by the text whose centre lies inside it, unnamed rooms are "Habitación n", a big outline that contains two or more other shapes is discarded as the house wall, and the result is scaled so the whole floor is 1200 cm on its longest side starting at 0,0. Test: tests/home.test.ts › "un dibujo de Excalidraw se vuelve habitaciones con su nombre y la silueta de la casa se descarta"
- **AC-2.2** (US-2) Given a drawing with no usable shapes, when `home_plan_from_drawing` (or the window) runs, then it fails with "No encontré rectángulos en el dibujo: dibujá cada habitación como un rectángulo (con su nombre adentro)." and the plan is unchanged. [gap]
- **AC-2.3** (US-2) Given a `.excalidraw` path in the vault and an optional `floor` name, when `home_plan_from_drawing` runs, then the floor's rooms are replaced, the plan is saved (with HA areas), `source` is `excalidraw` with the path, and the tool returns the new `describePlan`. [gap]
- **AC-2.4** (US-2) Given a vault path that does not exist or is not a drawing, when the conversion runs, then it fails and nothing is saved (an unreadable file parses as an empty drawing, which yields AC-2.2). [gap]
- **AC-2.5** (US-2) [manual] In Casa → Plano with an empty floor, "Desde un dibujo de Excalidraw" lists the `.excalidraw` files of the active vault (or says there are none, with how to draw one); clicking one builds the plan; a failure shows in red under the header. Checked by drawing three rectangles with names in the vault and clicking.

### Cameras (US-6)
- **AC-6.1** (US-6) Given a camera entity, when a still is requested, then Home Assistant's camera proxy is called (20 s timeout), retried up to 5 more times 2 s apart (many cameras allow one video connection at a time), and finally fails with "La cámara no entregó la imagen (…)" advising to close the live view; an entity that is not a `camera.` fails with "Esa entidad no es una cámara." [gap]
- **AC-6.2** (US-6) Given `camera_look` with `camera` (name or room), optional `question` (default "¿Qué se ve? ¿Hay personas, animales o algo fuera de lo normal?") and optional `send_telegram`, when it runs, then it takes a still, saves it in the inbox (`<entity>-<timestamp>.jpg`), asks the vision model with "Cámara «name». <question>" and returns "name: answer"; if several cameras match it fails listing them; if none matches it lists the cameras that exist (kinds restricted to cameras). [gap]
- **AC-6.3** (US-6) Given the vision model fails, when `camera_look` runs with `send_telegram`, then the photo is still sent to Telegram (caption "name: (No pude analizar la imagen.)") and the result says "no pude analizar la imagen (error)". [gap]
- **AC-6.4** (US-6) Given `send_telegram` and Telegram not connected, then the result adds "(No pude mandar la foto por Telegram: no está conectado.)"; connected, it adds "(Foto enviada por Telegram.)" with a caption of at most 1000 characters. [gap]
- **AC-6.5** (US-6) Given `camera_photo`, when it runs, then the still is sent to Telegram without calling the vision model and the result is "Foto de name enviada por Telegram."; with Telegram not connected the photo is kept in the inbox and the result gives its path (not an error); a Telegram rejection is an error "Telegram no aceptó la foto: …". Test: tests/home.test.ts › "camera_photo manda la foto por Telegram sin pasar por la visión" 
- **AC-6.6** (US-6) `camera_look`, `camera_photo` and `camera_watch` have risk `write`: they ask according to the approval mode unless the person chose "Siempre" for the tool; `look_at_image` is `read`. [gap]
- **AC-6.7** (US-6) Given the house is turned off or not connected, when any plan/camera/scene/rule tool runs, then it fails with "La domótica está apagada: se enciende en Configuración → Casa." or "La casa no está conectada (state…)…" (`home_plan`, `home_plan_edit`, `home_plan_from_drawing` and `home_plan_from_image` only need the house turned on, not connected). Test: tests/home.test.ts › "con la domótica apagada o sin conexión explica qué hacer" 
- **AC-6.8** (US-6) [manual] Selecting a camera in the plan or the device list shows its live picture (MJPEG from Home Assistant); "Foto" pauses the live picture for 1.2 s, takes a still and shows it, "Volver al vivo" resumes; the panel shows no stream when the camera has no `entity_picture`. Checked with a real camera (Tapo) or the demo camera.

- **AC-6.18** (US-6) Given a paired phone with the cameras scope on, when it asks for a still, then it gets the snapshot bytes of a listed camera (at most 2 MB decoded); nothing is stored and a failing camera returns its existing cause, never a blank. ### Camera watches (US-6)
- **AC-6.9** (US-6) Given `camera_watch` action `start` with `camera` and `condition` (and `minutes` 1–360, default 60; `every_seconds` 10–900, default 60; `repeat` default false), when it runs, then a watch is created and saved, the first look happens about 1 s later, and the answer states the id, interval, duration and the maximum number of looks; without `camera` or `condition` it fails with "Para empezar necesito la cámara (camera) y qué buscar (condition).". [gap]
- **AC-6.10** (US-6) Given a watch is created directly, then the interval is at least 10 s and the duration between 1 minute and 6 hours, whatever is asked. [gap]
- **AC-6.11** (US-6) Given a running watch, at each look the still is sent to the vision model with the condition and asked for only `{"match": true|false, "note": "…"}`; an unparsable answer counts as no match with the first 120 characters as the note; the check counter and `lastNote` are updated. [gap]
- **AC-6.12** (US-6) Given a look matches, then the person is notified (desktop notification and Telegram with the photo) with title "<camera>: <condition>"; if `repeat` is false the watch ends, otherwise it continues but waits at least 120 s and five times the interval before looking again. [gap]
- **AC-6.13** (US-6) Given the end time passes, then at the next look the person is told "Terminó la vigilancia de <camera>" (with the number of looks and "No pasó nada.") and the watch is removed. [gap]
- **AC-6.14** (US-6) Given a look fails (camera, vision), then the watch keeps going until it expires and the last note is "Error: …" (160 characters); given the house is not connected the look is skipped without counting; two looks of one watch never overlap. [gap]
- **AC-6.15** (US-6) Given the app restarts with watches saved in `rules.json`, then each is rescheduled 5 s after start (and an expired one announces its end at that first check). [gap]
- **AC-6.16** (US-6) Given `camera_watch` actions `list` and `stop`, then `list` shows id, camera, condition, interval, looks, end time and last note ("No hay vigilancias activas." if none); `stop` stops the given id, or the only one, otherwise it asks which; an unknown id says "Esa vigilancia no existe.". [gap]
- **AC-6.17** (US-6) [manual] In Casa → Reglas a running watch is listed under "Vigilancias en curso" with its camera, condition, interval, looks and last note, and "Detener" ends it. Checked by starting a watch from chat.

### Modes (US-7)
- **AC-7.1** (US-7) Given `home_scene` with a name, then the scene is found by exact name (ignoring case/accents) or entity id, else the first whose name contains it, and is activated (`scene.turn_on`); an unknown name fails listing the modes ("se crean con home_modes" if none). Risk is `home`: it never asks. [gap]
- **AC-7.2** (US-7) Given `home_modes` action `create` with `devices` ({"luz cocina": {"state":"on","brightness_pct":30}}) and/or `from_current` (names whose current state, brightness, colour, temperature, position, percentage, volume are stored as they are), then a scene is saved in Home Assistant with id `alma_<time>` and scenes are reloaded; a target matching several devices applies to all of them; neither `devices` nor `from_current` fails with "Decime qué hace cada dispositivo…"; a missing name fails with "Falta el nombre del modo."; an existing mode with that name is replaced (same id). [gap]
- **AC-7.3** (US-7) Given `home_modes` actions `list`, `show` and `delete`, then `list` returns the names ("No hay modos." if none), `show` the scene's JSON definition, `delete` removes it; scenes that Home Assistant has in its own configuration (no `id` attribute) cannot be deleted ("viene de la configuración"). [gap]
- **AC-7.4** (US-7) `home_modes` has risk `write` (asks per the approval mode); `home_scene` has risk `home`. [gap]
- **AC-7.5** (US-7) [manual] Modes appear as buttons under the plan (when not editing), in the Reglas tab ("Modos", with the hint "Pedile a Alma: «armame un modo Noche…»" when empty) and in the Casa sidebar; pressing one activates it. Checked with the demo scene.

### Rules in Home Assistant (US-8)
- **AC-8.1** (US-8) Given `home_rules` action `create_automation` with `name`, `trigger` and `actions` (Home Assistant's own format; optional `condition`, `description`), then the automation is saved (mode `single`, id `alma_<time>`), Home Assistant validates it, and the tool answers "Automatización «name» creada (id …) y activa."; missing name/trigger/actions fails with "Una automatización necesita name, trigger y actions."; a rejection by Home Assistant surfaces as the tool's error. [gap]
- **AC-8.2** (US-8) Given `list`, `toggle` and `delete`, then `list` shows "[automática id]", "[con criterio id]" and "[vigilancia id]" lines ("No hay reglas." if none); `toggle` needs `id` and `enabled`, acts on a Home Assistant automation or a judgment rule; `delete` removes either; an unknown id fails with "No hay una regla con ese id."; watches cannot be toggled or deleted here (use `camera_watch`). [gap]
- **AC-8.3** (US-8) `home_rules` has risk `write`. Simple rules do not call any model when they fire (Home Assistant runs them). [gap]
- **AC-8.4** (US-8) [manual] The Reglas tab lists Home Assistant automations with an on/off switch, last triggered time ("Todavía no se disparó" if never) and a delete button that asks "¿Borrar «name»?" first; the list updates when automations change. Checked by creating one from chat.

### Rules that need judgment (US-9)

## 6. Business rules
- **BR-1** The plan is stored in `<dataDir>/home/plan.json` (units: centimetres, x right, y down); every plan read or written is sanitized: at least one floor, polygons of at least 3 points, unique ids, one position per device, integer coordinates. (src/coordinator/home/plan.ts)
- **BR-2** The empty plan has one floor "Planta baja" with id `piso-1`. (src/shared/home.ts)
- **BR-3** A drawing or an image is scaled so the whole floor measures 1200 cm on its longest side, anchored at 0,0. (src/shared/home.ts)
- **BR-4** In a drawing, a shape that contains two or more other shapes (by bounding box) is the house outline and is not a room; a closed line needs at least 4 points and its ends within 15 % of its size. (src/shared/home.ts)
- **BR-5** A vision model answers a floor plan on a 0–100 grid; the horizontal axis is multiplied by width/height of the picture. (src/shared/home.ts, src/coordinator/vision.ts)
- **BR-6** A picture may be read only from the inbox, the vault root or the working folder; JPG/JPEG/PNG/WebP/GIF only; at most 8 MB. (src/coordinator/vision.ts)
- **BR-7** The model that looks is chosen as in AC-5.3; it is called with effort `low`, no tools. Pictures never enter an agent's conversation. (src/coordinator/vision.ts) *Amended by [038](../038-image-models-create-and-see/spec.md) (2026-11-09): "as in AC-5.3" now means the AC-5.3 note above; effort `low`, no tools and pictures-as-text are unchanged (038 BR-8). Camera watches read the choice at each look, so acknowledging a provider takes effect on the next check with no restart (038 AC-4.7).*
- **BR-8** A still is tried up to 6 times, 2 s apart, 20 s timeout each. (src/coordinator/home/hub.ts)
- **BR-9** A camera watch looks at least every 10 s (tool: 10–900 s, default 60) and lasts 1–360 minutes (default 60); the first look is 1 s after starting, saved watches resume 5 s after start; after a match with `repeat`, the next look waits at least 120 s and five times the interval. (src/coordinator/home/triggers.ts, src/coordinator/tools/home.ts)
- **BR-10** A judgment rule's cooldown is at least 1 minute (default 10); a rule that is disabled never fires; the cooldown is measured from the last firing, which is stored in the rule. (src/coordinator/home/triggers.ts, src/shared/home.ts)
- **BR-11** A judgment rule compares only the state value of one entity; there is no limit on the number of rules, nor a global limit of runs per hour; the only throttle is each rule's cooldown. (src/coordinator/home/triggers.ts)
- **BR-12** Rules and watches are stored in `<dataDir>/home/rules.json` as `{ judgment: [], watches: [] }`; simple rules and scenes live in Home Assistant (ids `alma_<base36 time>`). (src/coordinator/home/triggers.ts, src/coordinator/home/hub.ts)
- **BR-13** Tool risks: `home_plan` read; `home_plan_edit`, `home_plan_from_drawing`, `home_plan_from_image`, `home_scene` home; `camera_look`, `camera_photo`, `camera_watch`, `home_modes`, `home_rules` write; `look_at_image` read. `home` never asks (it is blocked only in read-only approval mode); `write` follows the approval mode and "Siempre permitir". (src/coordinator/tools/home.ts, src/coordinator/approval.ts)
- **BR-14** The house tools (and the house guide in the system prompt) are offered only when the house is turned on and the agent is not working in a company; `look_at_image` is offered to all agents. (src/coordinator/agent.ts)
- **BR-15** A judgment rule's run uses the agent set in Configuración → Casa (default agent otherwise) in a new conversation, and auto-approves only `camera_look`. (src/coordinator/index.ts)
- **BR-16** A notice from the house reaches the desktop (always, even with the window focused) and, if connected, Telegram (with the photo when there is one). (src/coordinator/index.ts)
- **BR-17** Camera stills saved by `camera_look` and `camera_photo` go to `<dataDir>/inbox/<entity>-<timestamp>.jpg`. (src/coordinator/home/index.ts)

- **BR-18** Remote clients reach cameras only through the remote channel: stills served to a paired phone come from the live snapshot and are never stored; only listed cameras are visible (spec 026).

## 7. Constraints and risks
- **Layers touched.** shared: `src/shared/home.ts` (plan geometry, `planFromDrawing`, `planFromVision`, `extractJson`, rule types, `ruleMatches`, `inWindow`), `src/shared/excalidraw.ts`, request/event types in `src/shared/types.ts` (`home.plan.get|set|fromDrawing`, `home.camera.snapshot`, `home.rules`, `home.rule.toggle|delete`; events `home.plan`, `home.rules`). coordinator: `src/coordinator/home/plan.ts`, `triggers.ts`, `index.ts` (`HomeService`: `savePlan`, `planFromDrawing`, `saveStill`), `hub.ts` (snapshots, scenes, automations), `src/coordinator/vision.ts`, `src/coordinator/tools/home.ts`, `src/coordinator/tools/telegram.ts` (`image_path`), `src/coordinator/index.ts` (`launchHomeRule`, `notifyHome`, `saveInboxImage`), `src/coordinator/agent.ts` (tool registration, `HOME_GUIDE`). renderer: `src/renderer/src/home/{HomeView,FloorPlan,RulesPanel,DevicePanel,useHome,homeLib}.tsx|ts` and `home.css`.
- **Persistence.** `<dataDir>/home/plan.json` (pretty JSON, atomic write); `<dataDir>/home/rules.json`; stills and received pictures in `<dataDir>/inbox/`; scenes and automations in Home Assistant. Old or damaged files are read leniently (see AC-10.1, AC-10.4, AC-9.11).
- **Tools and risk.** See BR-13. Messages between layers: window → coordinator requests listed above; coordinator → window `home.plan` after every plan save and auto-placement, `home.rules` after every rule change and when Home Assistant's automations change.
- **Security and privacy.** A camera still and a plan photo are sent to the configured vision provider (a third party, outside the machine); the person chooses it in Configuración → Casa *(amended by [038](../038-image-models-create-and-see/spec.md), 2026-11-09: now chosen in Configuración → Imágenes; a provider saved there without a model receives nothing until the person confirmed it by name)*. Stills are sent to Telegram only if asked or when a watch matches. The inbox holds personal pictures; `erasePersonalFiles` empties it ([../022-security-and-privacy/spec.md](../022-security-and-privacy/spec.md)). A woken agent runs without the person watching: only `home`/`read`/`memory` tools and `camera_look` run unasked; other `write` tools wait for an approval.
- **Cost.** Each look at a camera or plan is a vision call; its token usage is returned by the model call but not recorded anywhere for the house.
- **Platform.** Windows paths in `loadPicture` are compared case-insensitively. The window needs the engine ready to show the plan; the plan tools work with the house only turned on. No tests of the renderer exist; its behavior is `[manual]`.

# 007 — Empresas

## 1. Summary
A **company** is a team of agents that runs a business for the person. Each company has its own vault folder, an org chart (every position is an agent), a task board, meetings, a monthly budget and optionally its own mailbox and WhatsApp number. The person is the "Directorio" (the board) at the top of the chart. Agents sleep until there is something concrete: a task assigned to them, a review, a meeting, a routine, a question from a colleague or a message from the person. Work moves through tasks (assign, deliver, review, approve), anything that leaves the company is checked first by the agent's boss and then by the person, and spend is estimated per month with a cap that pauses agents when reached. The Empresas screen shows the board, the company's chats, meetings, org chart and activity; teams are created and edited in Configuración → Equipos.

## 2. Problem and goal
One agent cannot run a whole business role by role. The person wants to define roles, give a goal, and have several agents divide the work, check each other, meet, and only come back to the person for decisions and anything that goes outside — without agents polling a model (cost) and without an unbounded bill. Done means: a company can be created from a template or blank, its agents work on their own from tasks and meetings, deliveries are reviewed up the chain, outgoing actions pass boss then person, spend is capped monthly, and the person can watch and intervene from one screen.

## 3. Scope
**In:**
- The company model: company, members (positions), org chart rules (chain of command, direct reports, colleagues, loops), templates, default agent per position, agent names.
- Tasks: statuses, creation, assignment, delivery, review, revision limit, comments, escalation, who wakes whom.
- Meetings: scheduling, preparation, live turns, user interventions, minutes, tasks created from minutes.
- Budgets: estimated spend, prices, per-company and per-member monthly caps, warnings, what pauses.
- The seven agent tools in `src/coordinator/tools/company.ts`; the company-agent context in the system prompt; auto-approved tools inside a company; the boss → person approval chain for `publish` tools (the policy lives in `src/coordinator/approval.ts`, owned by 004).

## 4. Users and stories
- **US-1** As the person, I want to create a company from a template (or blank) with a vault, a mission and goals, so that I get a ready team without configuring each agent.
- **US-2** As the person, I want to edit the org chart (add, remove, re-parent positions, dotted lines) and each position's agent, so that the team matches my business.
- **US-3** As the person, I want to give a task to a member and have them do it on their own, so that I do not drive each step.
- **US-4** As a boss agent, I want to assign tasks to my team and review what they deliver, so that work is divided and checked.
- **US-5** As a member agent, I want to deliver, block, ask and escalate, so that I can finish or hand up what I cannot decide.
- **US-6** As the person, I want to review deliveries addressed to me (approve or request changes) and comment on any task, so that I stay in control.
- **US-7** As the person, I want meetings where members prepare, discuss by turns, and leave minutes and tasks, so that group decisions become work.
- **US-8** As the person, I want a monthly budget per company and per member, warnings, and work that pauses at the cap, so that cost is bounded.
- **US-9** As the person, I want the company to have its own mailbox and WhatsApp and for anything leaving the company to be checked by the agent's boss and then by me, so that nothing goes out unreviewed.
- **US-10** As the person, I want to watch what the team is doing (activity, who is working, spend) and talk to any member, so that I can follow and intervene.
- **US-11** As the maintainer, I want the org-chart, task, meeting and budget rules in pure/injected code, so that they are testable without a model.

## 6. Business rules
- **BR-1** The board is the id `user`; the system is `system`; any other actor is a member id. (src/shared/company.ts, src/shared/types.ts)
- **BR-2** A member reports to another member or to the board (`reportsTo: null`); a position may not report to itself or to someone below it. `collaborates` is a dotted line that is symmetric for asking. (src/shared/company.ts)
- **BR-3** "Colleagues" a member can ask = its boss, its direct reports and its dotted-line peers (both directions). `ask_colleague` also accepts any subordinate at any depth. `assign_task` only accepts subordinates at any depth. `schedule_meeting` accepts boss, colleagues and subordinates. (src/shared/company.ts, src/coordinator/company/engine.ts, src/coordinator/tools/company.ts)
- **BR-4** Task statuses: `todo`, `in_progress`, `review`, `done`, `blocked`; labels «Por hacer», «En curso», «En revisión», «Hecho», «Trabada». (src/coordinator/company/engine.ts)
- **BR-5** A task wakes its assignee only when `todo`, the assignee is a member (not `user`), is not already working and is not over budget; one run per member at a time. The check runs on every change and every 30 s. (src/coordinator/company/engine.ts)
- **BR-6** A task may come back from review at most 2 times; the third send-back blocks it and notifies the person. (`MAX_REVISIONS = 2`, src/coordinator/company/engine.ts)
- **BR-7** Review rights: the person, the task's creator, or any boss in the assignee's chain of command. (src/coordinator/company/engine.ts)
- **BR-8** A delivery created by the board is reviewed by the person; one created by a member is reviewed by that member's agent (a run); one created by `system` or by a member that no longer exists is not reviewed automatically. (src/coordinator/company/engine.ts)
- **BR-9** A review run that finds its creator busy retries every 15 s; the creator over budget stops the review. (src/coordinator/company/engine.ts)
- **BR-10** Engine timers: check every 30 s (`TICK_MS`), first check 8 s after start; meeting preparation starts `prepHours` hours before `at`. (src/coordinator/company/engine.ts)
- **BR-11** Meeting turns: max 120 words per turn (prompt), no tools, effort `low`; cap `min(60, agenda × (others + 1) + 6)` turns; speaking order: the chair opens; for each agenda item (from the second on, the chair first announces it) every non-chair participant speaks once in participants order, then the chair closes it. (src/coordinator/company/engine.ts, src/coordinator/index.ts)
- **BR-12** (The `Reuniones` folder name and the section headings are the `es` names; a new file takes the current `language`'s names and an existing folder under either language's name is used: spec 025 BR-23.) Meeting minutes are written to `Reuniones/<YYYY-MM-DD> <topic, max 60 chars, \ / : * ? " < > | → ->.md` in the company vault with Summary, Decisions, Tasks, per-participant Preparation and Transcription. (src/coordinator/company/engine.ts)
- **BR-13** Agreed actions become tasks created by the chair; the model's JSON is accepted only for actions whose owner participates. (src/coordinator/company/engine.ts)
- **BR-14** Meeting preparation and the live meeting are cheap by design: the live meeting uses no tools; preparation runs the agent with tools but forbids asking colleagues. (src/coordinator/company/engine.ts)
- **BR-15** Spend is an estimate from provider list prices; one price per provider, not per model. Month key `YYYY-MM` (local time); a new month starts at zero. (src/shared/company.ts)
- **BR-16** Caps: company cap `budgetUsd` (0 = none) and member cap `budgetUsd` (0 = only the company's). A new company starts at USD 20. Warnings at 80 % and 100 %, once per month per cap. (src/coordinator/company/engine.ts, src/renderer/src/companies/TeamsSettings.tsx)
- **BR-17** Company agents: `allowMcp` false by default, no home tools, they do not delegate with `delegate_task`, the user's own agents never delegate to them, and they run with the company's vault as working folder. (src/shared/company.ts, src/coordinator/agent.ts)
- **BR-18** Inside a company `write_file`, `edit_file`, `office_create`, `office_edit`, `generate_image`, `compose_image` do not ask (`COMPANY_AUTO_APPROVE`); `publish` tools always ask, first the boss, then the person. (src/coordinator/agent.ts, src/coordinator/approval.ts)
- **BR-19** The company mailbox password is encrypted at rest like API keys and is never sent to the window. (src/main/settings.ts)
- **BR-20** Activity log keeps 300 entries; the window gets 150. (src/coordinator/company/store.ts, src/coordinator/company/engine.ts)
- **BR-21** Notifications to the person (desktop plus Telegram): task delivered for the board's review, a task stuck after 2 revisions, a blocked/escalating member with no boss, a meeting starting, budget at 80 % and 100 %. (src/coordinator/company/engine.ts, src/coordinator/index.ts)
- **BR-22** `createTask` accepts the board (`user`) as assignee and never wakes it, but no tool or window action assigns to the board. (src/coordinator/company/engine.ts)

## 7. Constraints and risks
- **Layers touched** (see `docs/architecture.md`): `shared` (`company.ts`, company types in `types.ts`, request/event types, `defaults.ts`); `coordinator` (`company/engine.ts`, `company/store.ts`, `tools/company.ts`, wiring in `index.ts`, seat/context/auto-approve in `agent.ts`, boss review in `approval.ts`); `main` (`settings.ts` secrets, `index.ts` handler `settings:setCompanySecret`); `renderer` (`companies/CompaniesView.tsx`, `OrgChart.tsx`, `TeamsSettings.tsx`, `companies.css`, sidebar pane and mode in `App.tsx`, settings tab in `SettingsDialog.tsx`).
- **Persistence:** company definitions and agents in the settings file (`companies`, `agents` with `companyId`, `routines`); mailbox passwords in `companySecrets` (encrypted); board, meetings, activity (300) and monthly spend (one entry per month) plus the set of budget warnings already sent in `<dataDir>/companies/<companyId>.json` (JSON, indent 1, written through a `.tmp` file then renamed); company conversations are ordinary threads with channel `company`; a company's WhatsApp session in `<dataDir>/whatsapp-auth-<companyId>`; documents and minutes in the company vault folder.
- **Messages:** requests `company.snapshot`, `company.task.create|comment|review|move|delete`, `company.meeting.create|start|say|end|cancel`, `company.whatsapp.status|logout`; events `company.changed`, `company.whatsapp`; desktop API `settings.setCompanySecret(companyId, secret)`. `company.task.create` from the window always has creator `user`.
- **Tools and risk:** `assign_task`, `update_task`, `review_task`, `ask_colleague`, `escalate`, `schedule_meeting` are `memory`; `list_tasks` is `read`. `memory` and `read` never ask (approval policy), so a company agent can create work, meetings and escalations without any approval.
- **Security / privacy:** every member can write in its company vault without asking; outgoing mail, posts and WhatsApp (`publish`) go through boss then person; a company's WhatsApp and mailbox are separate accounts; no new third party. Entries for `docs/security/` belong to 022.
- **Performance / platform:** agents are woken by a 30 s timer; meeting speech is one call per turn; a company with many members can run many agents at once (one per member). Windows paths for vaults.

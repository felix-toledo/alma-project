## 1. Summary
From the phone app (spec 026/028) the person can attach pictures and files to a chat message: in the Chat tab (the shared remote thread) and, when they allowed the phone to continue conversations, in a conversation opened from the list. The phone sends each file to the PC on its own request, and the PC puts it through exactly the same checks and the same store as an attachment added at the desktop (spec 030): type and content re-checked on the bytes, secret-looking names refused, location data stripped from pictures, 30 days of life. Sending files is a new sub-switch under Chat on the PC, off at every turn-on and confirmed in a system dialog the page cannot click; the existing limits do not change, so a file sent from the phone is at most 700 000 bytes and a photo is shrunk on the phone to fit. The phone then shows each message's attachments by name, kind and size, never their content. Finally, opening a conversation on the phone counts as opening it for the 30-day clock of spec 030.

## 2. Problem and goal
The desktop composer can attach files since spec 030, but the phone's chat cannot: away from the PC the person who wants the agent to look at a photo of a receipt, a PDF or a log has to send it by another channel or wait. Spec 030 left two phone items open: the phone does not tell Alma that a conversation was opened (so a conversation read only from the phone could have its attachments swept while in use), and what the phone's thread view does with a message that carries attachments was never decided.

Facts the design respects (verified in code and specs):
- Request body cap 1 MB, photo cap 2 MB (responses), 60 requests per minute per device, 8000-character message, 2 held long polls, 30 s timeout (026 BR-31); a change of limit is a spec change (028 BR-11). Base64 inflates a file by 4/3, so the 2 MiB file of 030 BR-3 does not fit in one 1 MB request (a 2 MiB file is about 2.8 MB as base64).
- Spec 030's single authority for what may be stored: `classifyFile`, `stripMetadata`, the secret-name check, the name sanitiser and `AttachmentStore` (`src/shared/attach/*`, `src/coordinator/attachments/*`). It binds attachments to a conversation (`composeUserMessage`, which refuses an id of another conversation) and refuses a company conversation (030 AC-1.11).
- The remote layer already has: one security pipeline (device, scope, sub-switches, schema, limits, audit) for every transport including the relay (027); `ThreadsFlow.read/send` for conversations by id (028); `ChatFlow.send` for the shared thread (026); native confirmations decided by the pure `planRemoteChange` (026/028).
- The phone's thread views serialise only text blocks (028 BR-4, `thread-view.ts`); a message with no text is left out (028 AC-3.3, 026 AC-5.5). 028 BR-4 says attachments never leave the PC through the read operations.
- `ThreadStore.markOpened` and `AttachmentService` `threads.open` exist (030); nothing calls `markOpened` from the phone's `ThreadsFlow.read` yet (030 tasks T32).
- The phone keeps nothing but its key and the app shell (026 BR-32, 028 BR-26).

Done means: with the sub-switch on, a person can attach photos and documents from the phone and send them with or without text, into the Chat tab or a conversation they may continue, and the agent can read them exactly as if they came from the desktop; every refusal names a concrete cause; with the sub-switch off, remote access off, the phone revoked or the PC unreachable, nothing is stored and the phone says why; the phone sees what was attached to a message by name only; and a conversation opened on the phone does not lose its attachments to the sweep while in use.

## 3. Scope
**In:**
- Three narrow-API operations: put one file to the Chat tab's shared thread, put one file to a conversation by id, remove a pending file; and the chat and conversation sends gain the list of pending file ids.
- The sub-switch "Send files from the phone" (settings, planRemoteChange, native confirmation with the provider notice, status, the PC tab text).
- Phone limits (BR-4), the phone's file types (BR-5), the on-phone photo shrinking, the secret-name refusal, the pending-upload rules (cap, expiry, cleanup).
- The phone's thread views carrying `{name, kind, size}` per attachment, and attachment-only messages.
- Phone half of 030 AC-6.7: `lastOpenedAt` set by the first read of a conversation by id.
- Phone half of 030 AC-8.1: the characterization test that the phone's thread view never serialises the stored `attachments` field, plus the projection of BR-13.
- The PWA: attach button, chips, errors, es/en text, touch look.
- Audit of uploads and of sends that carry attachments; docs in the same PR as the implementation.

**Out (and why):**
- Thumbnails, previews and downloads of attachments on the phone; opening an attachment on the phone: the phone sees names only (BR-13); a later spec if wanted.
- Chunked or resumable uploads and any change to the 1 MB, 2 MB or 60-per-minute limits (BR-3, BR-4): files above the phone limit are sent from the PC.
- A step-up or an approval card for sending a file (BR-9).
- Acknowledging the provider notice from the phone (BR-11) and a phone "attach anyway" for secret-looking names (BR-12).
- Attachments in company conversations (030 BR-24), in earlier remote threads other than by the by-id route, and any other channel (Telegram, WhatsApp, voice): unchanged.
- The phone reading, listing or fetching a stored attachment, or any route that names an attachment other than to remove the phone's own pending one.
- Vault files, camera pictures and notes as attachments from the phone (028 BR-27: no "attach this note").
- Sending a file out to the conversation's channel (028 BR-34: nothing goes outward because the phone continued it).

## 4. Users and stories
- **US-1** As a person away from the PC, I want to attach a photo or a document to my message from the phone, so that the agent can look at it without me going to the PC.
- **US-2** As the owner, I want sending files from the phone to be its own switch, off until I turn it on and confirmed on the PC, so that a lost or stolen phone cannot put files on my PC unless I allowed it.
- **US-3** As the owner, I want the same checks on a file from the phone as on one from the desktop, done on the PC, so that the phone is never the guard.
- **US-4** As a person, I want to know what was refused and why (too big, wrong type, looks secret, switch off, PC unreachable), so that I never think a file was sent when it was not.
- **US-5** As a person, I want to see on the phone what was attached to each message, by name, in the Chat tab and in conversations, so that I can follow what the agent was given.
- **US-6** As the owner, I want what was uploaded but never sent to disappear quickly and when the phone is cut off, and sent files to follow the same 30-day life as any attachment, so that nothing lingers.
- **US-7** As the owner, I want opening a conversation on the phone to count as opening it, so that its attachments are not swept while I am reading it there.
- **US-8** As the owner or a reviewer, I want every upload and every send with attachments on the record by device, size and kind and never by name or content, so that I can tell what a phone put on my PC.
- **US-9** As the maintainer, I want the phone to reuse spec 030's rules and store, the security layer to be changed deliberately and visibly, and the docs updated in the same PR, so that no second, weaker path to the attachment store exists.

- **AC-1.3** (US-2) Given the Chat scope is off, then the sub-switch cannot be on: turning Chat off turns it off, and a settings document with the sub-switch on and Chat off is read as off, like the continue sub-switch without History (028 AC-1.2). [gap]
- **AC-1.4** (US-2) Given the native confirmation, then its text says that files sent from the phone are kept on the PC for 30 days and read by the model provider of the agent that answers, and names the providers (the agent of the remote chat and, since pictures go to the vision model, its provider); on "Allow" those providers are recorded as acknowledged in the same list as the desktop notice (030 AC-3.1, `attachments.noticeAcknowledged`); on "Cancel" nothing is saved or acknowledged. [gap] *Amended by [038](../038-image-models-create-and-see/spec.md) (2026-11-09), wording only: "its provider" is the provider the looks would use under the see setting and the acknowledgement gate (038 BR-14, AC-4.1, AC-4.7); the confirmation logic is unchanged.*
- **AC-1.5** (US-2) Given the coordinator sends `settings.patch` with the sub-switch, when `main` receives it, then it is ignored (026 AC-1.5 extended; the whole `remote` section is stripped today and the test proves the new key too); and the tool registry has no tool that reads or changes the sub-switch (026 AC-1.6 extended). [gap]
- **AC-1.6** (US-2) Given the sub-switch is off, when a device puts a file, or sends a message that carries file ids, then it is refused with `attach-off` (the phone shows `remote.pwa.attach.off`), nothing is stored, no run starts, and nothing is read from disk. A text-only send is not affected. [gap]
- **AC-1.7** (US-2) Given the Chat scope is off, when a device puts a file to the shared thread, then it is refused as `scope-off`; given the target is a conversation by id, then History must be on (`scope-off`) and the continue-conversations sub-switch on (`write-off`), as for a by-id send (028 AC-1.1), and the Files sub-switch on. The verdict comes from the pure authorization function and is the same through any transport. [gap]
- **AC-1.8** (US-2) Given an unknown, revoked or idle-expired device, when it calls any new operation, then it gets the usual refusal (026 AC-5.3), the same for every case. [gap]
- **AC-1.9** (US-2) Given the status operation the Settings screen reads, then it also returns the on or off state of the Files sub-switch (counted only while Chat is on) and nothing else new (028 AC-6.11: a test lists the keys); the "what this phone can do" list shows "Send files" on or off. [gap]

### Putting a file (US-1, US-3, US-4)
- **AC-2.1** (US-1) Given the sub-switch on, the target allowed (AC-1.7), a file of at most 700 000 bytes that passes spec 030's rules, when a device puts it, then the PC stores it through the 030 store function (the same one the desktop uses), bound to the target conversation, answers its id, original name, kind and size, and sends nothing to any model provider (030 AC-1.1). [gap]
- **AC-2.2** (US-3) Given the bytes, when they are put, then the PC alone decides type and content by 030's `classifyFile` on the bytes it received (a `.png` that is an executable, an SVG, HTML, an archive, a file with no extension, a text file with a NUL byte in its first 8000 bytes, an empty file are refused); the answer names the reason, nothing is stored, and the phone's own check or `accept` filter is never what protects it. [gap]
- **AC-2.3** (US-3) Given 700 000 bytes, then it passes the size rule; given 700 001, then it is refused as too big and nothing is stored; the count is the real decoded byte count, not the claimed `size`; a body with bad base64, an empty file, a claimed size different from the bytes, extra keys, a missing field, or a request over 1 MB is rejected at the boundary before any handler runs (026 AC-5.4). [gap]
  *Clarified 2026-11-09 by the orchestrator after verification: the schema no longer bounds the base64 length below the transport cap (1 MB, 026 AC-5.4), so that anything over 700 000 decoded bytes always answers `too-large` instead of a schema rejection; the code change is being made now.*
- **AC-2.4** (US-3, US-4) Given a file whose name matches a secret-looking pattern of 030 BR-20 (`.env*`, `*.pem`, `id_rsa*`, `passwords.*`…), when it is put, then it is refused with the reason `secret-name`, nothing is stored, and no confirmation to override it exists on the phone (BR-12). [gap]
- **AC-2.5** (US-3) Given a JPEG, PNG or WebP picture with location or camera metadata, when it is put, then the stored copy has none (030 AC-3.3), whatever the phone did to it; a malformed picture is refused (030 AC-3.4) and never stored. [gap]
- **AC-2.6** (US-3) Given a company conversation, an unknown id or a deleted one as the target, when a file is put, then the answer is `gone`, identical for all three (028 AC-3.4), and nothing is stored. [gap]
- **AC-2.7** (US-3) Given the target conversation has a run in progress (started on the PC, by another surface or by a phone), when a device puts a file, then it is refused as `busy` and nothing is stored, as attaching is disabled during a run at the desktop (030 AC-1.10). [gap]
- **AC-2.8** (US-3) Given the provider that would read the file (the target conversation's agent and, for a picture, the vision provider) is not in the acknowledged list (030 AC-3.1, AC-1.4), when a device puts a file, then it is refused as `notice-needed`, nothing is stored, and the phone shows `remote.pwa.attach.noticeNeeded`; the phone cannot acknowledge anything. [gap] *Amended by [038](../038-image-models-create-and-see/spec.md) (2026-11-09), wording only: "the vision provider" is the see setting of Configuración → Imágenes subject to 038 BR-14 (the fallback agent's provider when it is empty or not yet acknowledged); the refusal logic is unchanged.*
- **AC-2.9** (US-3) Given a device already has 5 pending uploads (BR-7), when it puts a sixth, then it is refused as `too-many` and nothing is stored; given a pending upload is removed, sent, or expired, then the slot is free again. [gap]
- **AC-2.10** (US-4) Given the device exceeds 60 requests in a minute or the PC does not finish within 30 seconds, when it puts a file, then the existing refusals apply (026 AC-10.2, AC-10.4); uploads add no limit of their own beyond AC-2.9, and `REMOTE_LIMITS` is unchanged. [gap]
- **AC-2.11** (US-3) Given storing fails on the PC (disk full, store not writable), when a device puts a file, then the answer is a failure naming `storage`, no partial file or record is left (030 AC-2.5), and the phone shows `remote.pwa.attach.storage`. [gap]

### Pending uploads and cleanup (US-6)
- **AC-3.1** (US-6) Given a file stored by a put and not yet sent, then it is a pending upload of that device for that conversation; it is deleted at once (file and record) when the device removes it, when it expires 15 minutes after it was put, when the device is revoked, forgets the PC or expires idle, when remote access is turned off or the tray kill switch is used, when the sub-switch or the Chat scope is turned off, and, for a Chat-tab upload, when "New conversation" replaces the shared thread. A sent attachment is not deleted by any of these: it follows spec 030. [gap]
- **AC-3.2** (US-6) Given the remove operation with an id that is not a pending upload of the calling device (another device's, a sent attachment, an unknown or malformed id), then nothing is deleted and the answer is the same as for a successful removal, so it reveals nothing. [gap]
- **AC-3.3** (US-6) Given the PC restarts while uploads are pending, then they are no longer pending (nothing is bound), their files stay until spec 030's 30-day sweep, and a send naming their ids is refused (AC-4.2). [gap]
- **AC-3.4** (US-6) Given "Borrar mi historial", then the whole attachment store goes as in spec 030 (030 AC-5.2), pending uploads included; "Exportar mis datos" excludes attachments (030 AC-5.5). [gap]

### Sending with attachments (US-1, US-4)
- **AC-4.1** (US-1) Given 1 to 5 ids of this device's pending uploads for the target conversation, and text that may be empty or up to 8000 characters, when a device sends into the Chat tab's thread or into a conversation by id, then the user message is composed by spec 030's `composeUserMessage` with the structured attachments, committed first (004 AC-8.9), the run starts through the same entry the PC uses, the model reads the 030 heading and reference lines (030 AC-4.1), and the uploads stop being pending. With no ids the send is exactly as today (a blank text is still refused). [gap]
- **AC-4.2** (US-4) Given an id that is not a pending upload of this device for this target (unknown, expired, another device's, another conversation's, already sent, malformed), or more than 5 ids, or a duplicate, when the device sends, then the send is refused as `unavailable`, nothing runs, no message is committed, the device's other pending uploads are untouched, and the answer is the same whatever the cause. [gap]
  *Clarified 2026-11-09 by the orchestrator after verification: the schema no longer bounds the id-list length below the transport cap, so that more than 5 ids always answer `unavailable` (same answer as every other cause) instead of a schema rejection; the code change is being made now.*
- **AC-4.3** (US-4) Given a send that is refused for any other reason (the conversation is busy, the device has a run active, the text is over 8000 characters, the sub-switch was turned off, the provider notice is no longer acknowledged because the conversation's agent changed), then nothing runs and the pending uploads stay pending until they expire; the phone keeps the text and the chips. [gap]
- **AC-4.4** (US-1) Given an attachment-only message from the phone, then it is accepted and the run starts; a new conversation cannot be started by it (the phone never starts conversations with a chosen agent: 028 BR-32), and the title rule of 030 AC-2.7 applies where a thread is untitled. [gap]
- **AC-4.5** (US-1) Given a conversation of the Telegram, WhatsApp or voice kind, when the phone sends files into it, then nothing is sent out to that channel (028 AC-3.13, BR-34). [gap]
- **AC-4.6** (US-1) Given a run started from the phone with attachments, when the agent reads one with `read_attachment` (risk `read`, never asks: 030 AC-4.6), then it works as in a desktop run; every other tool of the run keeps its risk and approvals as in 028 BR-21; the phone's approval cards are unchanged (004 AC-12.x). [gap]

### What the phone sees (US-5)
- **AC-5.1** (US-5) Given a message with attachments, when the phone reads the Chat tab's state or a conversation (`threads.read`), then the message carries, besides its text, a list with each attachment's name, kind and size and nothing else: never the id, the stored name or path, the bytes, a thumbnail, the conversation id of the store or the `removed` state. [gap]
- **AC-5.2** (US-5) Given a stored message with the `attachments` field and a marker string in every field of an attachment (id, name, size), when its view is serialised for the phone, then the output contains the name, kind and size only, and not the id nor any other field; the existing behavior that the view carries text only (028 BR-4) is pinned by this test. [gap]
- **AC-5.3** (US-5) Given a message with attachments and no text, then it is listed (with an empty text and its attachments) in the Chat tab and in conversation reads, instead of being left out (028 AC-3.3 amended); a message with neither text nor attachments is still left out. [gap]
- **AC-5.4** (US-5) Given the 1 MB response cap, then the attachment lists count toward it (the cut-to-fit logic of the thread view sees them) and a page never exceeds it. [gap]
- **AC-5.5** (US-5) Given an attachment whose file was swept or deleted, then the phone still shows its name, kind and size as in AC-5.1; it does not learn whether the file still exists. [gap]
- **AC-5.6** (US-5) Given a message sent from the desktop, Telegram or a routine that has attachments, then the phone shows its attachments the same way when it reads that conversation (History scope on). [gap]

### Opening a conversation on the phone (US-7) — spec 030 AC-6.7, phone half
- **AC-6.1** (US-7) Given the History scope on and a reachable conversation, when a device reads it by id for the first time in an open (no `before`, no `since`), then the conversation's `lastOpenedAt` is set to now through the thread store's `markOpened`, which does not change `updatedAt`, the list order or retention. [gap]
- **AC-6.2** (US-7) Given a read with `since` (the held long-poll repeats) or with `before` (load earlier), a read that answers `gone` (unknown or company), or a listing, then `lastOpenedAt` is not set. [gap]
- **AC-6.3** (US-7) Given a conversation opened on the phone, then the 30-day clock of spec 030 restarts for its attachments (030 AC-6.3) and one whose file was already deleted does not come back. [gap]
- **AC-6.4** (US-7) Given the Chat tab's state poll of the shared thread, then it does not set `lastOpenedAt` (a send into it does, through the run entry: 030 AC-6.7). [gap]

### Audit and privacy (US-8)
- **AC-7.1** (US-8) Given a put that reaches the PC (stored or refused for the file), when handled, then exactly one entry is written through the existing `audit()` with `actor: 'remote'`, `detail.deviceId`, the action `remote.attachments.put`, the outcome, and, when the file was stored, its kind and size as numbers; the store's own entry (030 AC-5.7) stays. A remove is audited as a change; a send that carries attachments is audited as today with the number of attachments added to its detail. [gap]
- **AC-7.2** (US-8) Given any audit entry or log line for these operations, then the `remote.attachments.*` entries (the phone channel's own) carry identifiers of the device, outcomes, kinds, sizes and counts only: never a file name, content, text, id of an attachment, secret or base64. The attachment store's own entries (`attachment.store|remove|refuse|sweep`, 030 AC-5.7, AC-7.1) keep the attachment id, kind and size exactly as 030 defines them: that id is an unguessable capability kept only in the local audit file and never sent to the phone. A test feeds marker strings through every new operation and scans the `remote.attachments.*` trail (026 AC-7.2). [gap]
  *Clarified 2026-11-09 by the orchestrator on the owner's delegation after verification: AC-7.1 keeps the store's 030 entry (with id) and the earlier wording of this criterion forbade any attachment id in any entry for these operations, which conflicted; the ban now applies to the phone channel's own `remote.attachments.*` entries only. Owner may veto.*
- **AC-7.3** (US-8) Given a scope or sub-switch refusal, a failed authentication or a refusal of the file on a new operation, then it is audited as in 026 AC-7.5 (`denied`, throttled one per minute, no names); and given the trail cannot be written, then the operation is not interrupted (026 AC-7.4). [gap]
- **AC-7.4** (US-8) Given any new response, then it carries `Cache-Control: no-store`, the service worker does not cache it, and the phone keeps no file, chip or attachment list in storage: the bytes are dropped from memory once uploaded (026 BR-32, 028 BR-26). [gap]
- **AC-7.5** (US-8) Given a put or a send with attachments, then neither the channel nor the PWA contacts any host other than the PC (with the relay selected, the relay carries the file as a sealed frame it cannot open: 027), and nothing goes to a model provider until a run reads the file (026 AC-9.1). [gap]
- **AC-7.6** (US-8) [manual] The remote-activity list on the PC (`remote.activity.title`) shows an upload and a send with attachments with the device name, when and what. Checked after a phone uploads, sends and a stranger is refused.

### The phone app (US-1, US-4)
- **AC-8.1** (US-1) Given a picked file, then a pure function in `shared` decides what the phone does before sending: a picture within the limit is sent as is; a picture over the limit is shrunk and re-encoded as JPEG on the phone until it fits and is sent; a picture that cannot be made to fit, a GIF over the limit, or any other file over the limit is refused on the phone with the file's name and `remote.pwa.attach.tooBig`; a type outside the 030 allow-list is refused on the phone with `remote.pwa.attach.type`; more than 5 chips are refused with `remote.pwa.attach.tooMany`; a picture re-encoded on the phone is named `imagen-<timestamp>.jpg` (030 AC-1.5). The PC re-checks all of it (AC-2.2, AC-2.3). [gap]
- **AC-8.2** (US-1) Given the Chat tab and an open conversation that may be continued, then the composer has an attach button (`remote.pwa.attach.button`) that opens the phone's own file chooser, which offers camera, photo library and files, allows several files, and cancelling changes nothing; the button is reachable with the keyboard and a screen reader and is at least 44 by 44 px. - **AC-8.3** (US-1) Given picked files, then each becomes a chip with the file name, a state (preparing, uploading, ready, failed) and a remove X; chips upload one at a time, in order; the send button stays disabled while any chip is preparing or uploading; the X on a ready chip asks the PC to remove the pending upload and, on an uploading one, aborts the request. [gap]
- **AC-8.4** (US-4) Given the sub-switch is off, then the attach button is shown disabled with `remote.pwa.attach.off` as the reason (as a destination whose scope is off stays visible: 028 BR-36); given the sub-switch is turned on or off on the PC while the app is open, then the next request changes it without a reload (028 AC-6.5). - **AC-8.5** (US-4) Given any refusal of the PC or of the phone's own check, then the chip shows exactly one concrete dictionary key (§6.1) naming the file and the cause; a pure mapping from every reason to one key has a test, and a reason without a key fails it (028 AC-6.3). `remote.pwa.retry` is offered only where trying again can help (the PC unreachable, a failed upload), not for a type, size, secret name, switch off or notice. - **AC-8.6** (US-4) Given the PC is unreachable, asleep or the connection drops during an upload or a send, then the phone shows `remote.pwa.pcUnreachable`, nothing is queued or replayed later, the typed text and the chips stay, and a chip whose upload did not finish is marked failed so the person retries it by hand. - **AC-8.8** (US-5) Given the Chat tab and conversations, then a message with attachments shows its attachments under the text as name, a kind icon and size, no thumbnail and no link; an attachment-only message shows just them. [manual] Checked with a picture, a PDF and a text file.

## 6. Business rules
Rules the code must obey. Every rule marked (planner) is a decision the planner took on the owner's delegation ("avanza con tus recomendaciones", 2026-11-09), preferring the most conservative option; the owner may veto any of them (§8).

- **BR-1** (026 BR-1, BR-7; 028 BR-2) The new operations are part of the same remote channel: a closed list, a strict schema each, session authentication, the pure authorization function. Network position never authenticates. The phone never touches the attachment store directly: it only asks the PC to put or remove a pending upload of its own.
- **BR-2** (planner, Q1) Sending files is a sub-switch "Send files from the phone" under the Chat scope, not a new scope. It is off at every turn-on, needs its own native confirmation to be turned on, none to be turned off, and cannot be on while Chat is off. A put into a conversation by id also needs History and the continue-conversations sub-switch (028 BR-18). The sub-switch is changed only on the PC, through `main`; the coordinator's `settings.patch` and every tool cannot touch it (026 BR-11).
- **BR-3** (planner, Q2) The limits of 026/028 do not change. One file is one request, so a file from the phone is at most 700 000 bytes (decimal). *Why: base64 inflates by 4/3, so 700 000 bytes is 933 336 characters, which with a 512-character name and the JSON around it stays under the 1 MB request cap with margin; a 2 MiB file (030 BR-3) cannot fit and chunking adds server-side upload state, partial files and a bigger attack surface, which the conservative reading avoids.* The per-file limit of 2 MiB and the 10 MiB per message of 030 still apply on the PC as the outer bounds.
- **BR-4** (planner, Q2) Photos are shrunk on the phone, not on the PC. A camera photo is several MB, so under 030 BR-14 (no automatic downscaling, picture over the limit refused) it would almost always be refused; the PWA therefore scales a picture over the limit down and re-encodes it as JPEG until it fits, or refuses it. This is a convenience; the PC trusts none of it and strips metadata and re-checks everything itself (AC-2.2, AC-2.5). The steps (maximum side, quality) are a plan matter; the rule is "fits or is refused, never sent larger".
- **BR-5** (planner, Q3) The phone may send what spec 030 accepts (BR-13: pictures PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF; text and source files; PDF; Word, Excel, PowerPoint), no narrower and no wider: `classifyFile` on the bytes the PC received is the only authority and there is no second list. The file chooser's `accept` list is a convenience. At most 5 files per message (030 BR-14); with 700 000 bytes each the 10 MiB total can never be reached.
- **BR-6** (planner, Q4) No step-up and no approval card for putting or sending a file. *Why: it is not a tool, so it has no `ToolRisk`; the file only lands in the attachment store as data (030 BR-2), is read by a model only when a run reads it with `read_attachment` (`read`, never asks), cannot be executed or opened from the phone, and is gated by a switch the owner turned on in a system dialog; the phone can already, with the continue sub-switch, make an agent work in a conversation with no step-up (028 BR-28), which is stronger than storing a file.* Anything the run then does keeps its own `ToolRisk` and approvals (028 BR-21).
- **BR-7** (planner, Q6, Q8, Q9) A put stores through spec 030's store and the file is a pending upload of the calling device until a send binds it. At most 5 pending uploads per device at a time. A pending upload expires 15 minutes after it was put, and is deleted at once with its record when removed, when the device is revoked, forgets the PC or expires idle, when remote access is turned off or the kill switch is used, when the sub-switch or the Chat scope is turned off, or when "New conversation" replaces the shared thread it belongs to. Pending state lives in memory: after a restart the files wait for the 30-day sweep (030 G15). Once a send binds it, an attachment belongs to the conversation and follows spec 030 alone (30 days from last opened, deleted with the conversation, erased with "Borrar mi historial", never exported). Revoking a phone does not delete what it already sent.
- **BR-8** (planner, Q10) A put into a company conversation, an unknown or a deleted one answers `gone`, the same for all (028 AC-3.4). A put into a conversation with a run in progress is refused as `busy`, as attaching is disabled during a run at the desktop (030 BR-23). A send keeps its own rules (one phone-started run per device, busy conversations: 028 BR-33).
- **BR-9** (planner, Q5, Q9) Audit: a put writes one `remote` entry `remote.attachments.put` with the device id, the outcome and, when stored, kind and size (numbers); never a name, id, content or text. A send with attachments is audited as today with the count. Rate: the 60 requests a minute per device of 026 BR-31 apply unchanged, plus the pending cap of BR-7; there is no new limit. A file refused or a failed upload counts as a request like any other.
- **BR-10** (planner, Q7) The phone's thread views (the Chat tab and conversation reads) carry, for a message with attachments, a list of name, kind and size, and nothing else of the attachment: no id (ids are capabilities for the model, 030 BR-24), no path, no bytes, no thumbnail, no removed state. A message with no text but with attachments is listed. This amends the "text only, attachments never leave" of 028 BR-4 in exactly this way and no other. *Why names only: the PC has no image decoder (030 plan), so a thumbnail would be the whole file, which counts against the 60 per minute limit and the 1 MB response cap; a file name is no more sensitive than the message text the History scope already shows.*
- **BR-11** (planner, Q11) The provider notice (030 AC-3.1) is never acknowledged from the phone, because the phone must not change settings. It is acknowledged at the PC: either by the native confirmation that turns the sub-switch on (AC-1.4), which names the providers it covers, or by the desktop notice of 030. A put or a send that would let an unacknowledged provider read the file is refused as `notice-needed`, checked at put and again at send (the conversation's agent or model may have changed).
- **BR-12** (planner, Q12) A secret-looking file name (030 BR-20, the name only: the phone gives no path) is refused from the phone with no override. *Why: a "confirm anyway" on the phone would be a flag the client asserts, so it would protect nothing; the person who really wants to send such a file does it from the PC, where the warning and the confirmation of 030 AC-3.2 exist.*
- **BR-13** (planner, Q7) See BR-10; the projection is produced by one pure function in `shared` used by the Chat tab state and by `threads.read`, so the two never differ.
- **BR-14** (planner, Q8) Offline and abort: nothing is queued, stored for later, or replayed (026 BR-33). An upload that does not complete stores nothing, because the PC reads the whole body before the handler runs; if the answer is lost after the file was stored, the phone retries by hand and the orphaned pending upload counts toward the cap of 5 until it expires. A chip the person removes while it uploads aborts the request; if the file was already stored, a best-effort remove follows and expiry is the backstop.
- **BR-15** (human 030 BR-16, planner Q-phone) Opening a conversation on the phone is the first read of it by id (no `before`, no `since`) by a device with the History scope; the reads that follow in the same open (long-poll repeats, load earlier), listings and the Chat tab's state poll do not count. It sets `lastOpenedAt` and nothing else (`updatedAt`, order and retention are untouched: 030 plan §4). A by-id send and a send into the shared thread count through the run entry (030 AC-6.7).
- **BR-16** (floor, 030 BR-10) The PC re-checks type, size and name on the bytes it receives; the phone's checks are a convenience, never the guard. Validation uses Zod at the boundary (Constitution IV).
- **BR-17** (floor, 030 BR-12; 026 BR-15) Secrets, file contents, attachment names and ids are not written to logs, audit entries or errors.
- **BR-18** (floor, 030 BR-2) An attachment from the phone is data, not instructions; nothing inside a file changes the approval policy or the tools offered.
- **BR-19** (floor, 026 BR-32; 028 BR-26) The phone stores no file, chip or attachment list; responses are `no-store`; bytes live in memory only until uploaded.
- **BR-20** (floor, 026 BR-3; 027) No new Alma server and no new third party. A file crosses the relay, when selected, only as sealed frames.
- **BR-21** (floor, 026 BR-20; 028 BR-9) UI text by dictionary key in the phone's es/en dictionary and the shared dictionaries, with parity tests. English is US English and Spanish rioplatense. UI work loads the `console-ui` and `umbra-ui` skills at the plan and implement stages; the plan names the components and tokens used and invents none.
- **BR-22** (floor, Constitution V) This spec adds no tool and no `ToolRisk`. The new narrow-API operations are not tool calls (028 BR-12).
- **BR-23** (planner, Q13) Interface: one attach button in the composer opens the phone's own file chooser (camera, photo library and files in a single sheet on both iOS and Android); chips show name and state; errors are concrete texts; no thumbnails; no sound; the same composer part serves the Chat tab and the conversation screen.

## 7. Constraints and risks
- **Layers touched** (see `docs/architecture.md`):
  - `shared`: `shared/attach` gains one file of pure phone rules (the phone limit, the file plan of AC-8.1, the pending-upload rules, the projection of BR-10, the refusal-to-key mapping); `shared/remote` (protected, below) gains the sub-switch in settings and `planRemoteChange`, the route specs and strict schemas, the refusals, the status shape and the thread-view wire type; dictionaries (shared and the PWA's own).
  - `coordinator/channels/remote` (protected, below): one new module for the put/remove/pending flow that calls spec 030's attachment service through an injected dependency; `threads.ts` gains the `opened` dependency; `chat.ts`, `threads.ts` and `channel.ts` carry the id list into the run entry; `thread-view.ts` carries the projection; `audit.ts` gains the new operations. `coordinator/index.ts` wires them (`ask` gains an optional list of attachment ids that reaches `composeUserMessage`).
  - `main`: the settings key and sanitiser, the native confirmation `main.remote.attachments` (and the acknowledgment it records, in `main`, from a pure function).
  - `renderer`: the "Remote access" tab row for the sub-switch (`ToggleRow` pattern of the continue sub-switch). `remote-client`: the composer part (attach button, chips, picker, shrinking), message attachment lines, Settings list, dictionaries.
- **Security / privacy:**
  - **This spec changes the spec-027-protected security layer** (`src/coordinator/channels/remote/*`, `src/shared/remote/*` as listed in `tests/support/remote-protected.json`). Expected protected files changed: `shared/remote/api.ts`, `authorize.ts`, `settings.ts`, `wire.ts`, `status.ts`, `confirmations.ts`, `coordinator/channels/remote/channel.ts`, `chat.ts`, `threads.ts`, `thread-view.ts`, `audit.ts`, `transport.ts` (new refusals' status codes), and the files that implement the pending cleanup on revoke/forget/off (`devices-admin.ts`, `runs.ts` or the new module); new files under those folders must be added to the manifest. **The manifest hashes must be updated deliberately, in the same change and in plain sight** (AC-9.4); `tests/remote-relay-contract.test.ts` and `tests/verify-027-optin-unchanged-channel.test.ts` must pass with them. None of the edits may be about the relay (that test checks it).
  - Attack surface: a stolen, unlocked, paired phone with Chat and the Files sub-switch on can put up to 5 files of 700 000 bytes pending (about 3.5 MB per device, 17.5 MB over the 5 devices) on the PC's disk, each expiring in 15 minutes unless sent; with a send it can make the agent read them, under the usual approvals, with the provider notice acknowledged. Controls: the sub-switch (off at every turn-on, native confirmation), the Chat scope, the allow-list of types re-checked on the bytes, the secret-name refusal, EXIF stripping, the pending cap and expiry, the 60-per-minute limit, the 15-day idle expiry, revoke, the tray kill switch, the audit trail. Residual: a file allowed by the list can contain hostile text (030 BR-2: the approval policy is the real guard); in "Permitir todo" mode a phone-started run's `exec` does not ask (028 BR-21, already documented).
  - New data flow: the owner's files travel phone → PC over Tailscale (or the relay as sealed frames); then, when a run reads one, PC → the model provider as with the desktop (030). No new third party. `docs/security/PRIVACY.md` (a file from the phone is kept 30 days on the PC; pending uploads 15 minutes; relay carries it sealed), `CONTROLS.md` (the sub-switch, pending cap and expiry, secret-name refusal, no override, names-only view), `RISKS.md` (R-14 row: the phone can put files on the PC; R-15 of 030 cross-referenced) change in the same PR.
  - The projection of BR-10 leaks attachment names to a phone with the History scope on (and the Chat tab); the id is never sent.
- **Performance / platform:** a 700 000-byte file is about 933 KB as base64 in a request under the 1 MB cap; the PC holds at most one such body per request; the relay's 4 MB frame cap and its 1 GB per day budget are not reached by this (027). Browser canvas re-encoding (shrinking) is memory-heavy on old phones: the plan processes one file at a time. Windows names: the stored name is hardened by 030's sanitiser, never the phone's.
- **UI components and tokens** (BR-21, the `umbra-ui` skill adapted to touch as the PWA already does in `pwa.css`): reuse the composer (`.pwa-compose`, `.pwa-dock`), buttons `.ghost` (attach), `.primary` (send), `.danger`, status lines `.pwa-note` / `.pwa-error` / `.pwa-status`, `.pwa-actions`, `.pwa-sr`, the states of `StateView`; Lucide icons at 20 px, stroke 1.75 (`Paperclip` for attach, `X` for remove, `Image` and `FileText` for kinds); tokens `--pwa-glass`, `--pwa-glass-hover`, `--pwa-border`, `--pwa-text`, `--pwa-accent`, `--pwa-danger`, `--pwa-r-pill`, `--pwa-r-sm`, `--pwa-dur`, `--pwa-ease`. The one explicit **extension**: a `.pwa-chip` class (pill, glass surface, 44 px high targets, one line with ellipsis, remove button) built only from the tokens above; no new color, spacing scale or font. On the PC tab the sub-switch row reuses the `console-ui` / `umbra-ui` `Toggle` already used for the other sub-switches.
